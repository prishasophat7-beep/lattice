# Pune Plant - Performance Report H1 2026 (January to June)

## Summary
Pune produced 61,240 good parts in H1 2026 against a target of 60,000. Overall equipment effectiveness (OEE) averaged 72.4 % against a target of 75 %. Line C was the main source of downtime because of the spindle failure on machine HX-07 in April (see the downtime table).

## Notes
- April, Line C: HX-07 spindle bearing failure; the machine was down for 9 days. The investigation found that the spindle warm-up program had been skipped on 14 of 20 working days in March, so the bearing replacement was not covered by the warranty.
- June, Line C: material shortage of 6061 aluminium bar stock from supplier Arvind Metals (late deliveries on 3 orders).
- Line B introduced quick-change fixtures in March, which cut its average setup time from 48 to 35 minutes.

# Helix Precision - Plant Safety Standard HPS-01 (Version 5, effective 1 March 2026)

## 1. Scope
This standard applies to all employees, contractors and visitors at the Pune and Chennai plants. Where a local rule is stricter, the stricter rule applies. Version 5 replaces Version 4 (2024); the main changes are listed in section 9.

## 2. Personal protective equipment (PPE)

### 2.1 PPE by area

| Area | Safety shoes | Safety glasses | Hearing protection | Gloves | Other |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Machining hall | Required | Required | Required above 85 dB(A) | Not while spindles run | - |
| Grinding bay | Required | Required + face shield | Required | Cut-resistant (level C) | Dust mask FFP2 |
| Heat treatment | Required | Required | - | Heat-resistant | Aluminized apron above 400 °C |
| Paint shop | Required | Goggles | - | Nitrile | Respirator A2P3 |
| Warehouse | Required | - | - | Work gloves | High-visibility vest |
| Offices | - | - | - | - | - |
| Quality lab | Required | Required | - | - | Lab coat |

### 2.2 Visitors
Visitors must be escorted at all times in production areas and wear safety shoes, safety glasses and a visitor vest. Visitors may not enter the heat treatment area or the paint shop. Visitors who are not wearing safety shoes may only use the marked visitor walkway.

### 2.3 Contractors
Contractors must complete the safety induction (45 minutes) before starting work and receive a contractor badge valid for 12 months. Contractors working at height or on electrical equipment also need a work permit (section 5) for each job.

## 3. Incident reporting

### 3.1 What to report
Report every injury, near miss, fire, spill of more than 5 liters of oil or chemicals, and any damage to a machine guard.

### 3.2 Deadlines

| Event | Report to | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Serious injury (hospital treatment, fracture, amputation) | Plant manager and EHS head by phone | Immediately, within 1 hour |
| Lost-time injury | EHS head | Within 4 hours |
| First-aid injury | Shift supervisor | Before the end of the shift |
| Near miss | Shift supervisor, through the HSE app | Within 24 hours |
| Fire (any size) | Fire control room (extension 333) | Immediately |
| Chemical spill above 5 liters | EHS head | Within 2 hours |

A written investigation report must be completed within 7 calendar days for lost-time and serious injuries, and within 14 days for near misses with a high potential severity.

### 3.3 Night shift
On the night shift (22:00 - 06:00) the shift supervisor acts for the plant manager for the first report. The plant manager must still be informed by 08:00.

## 4. Lockout / tagout (LOTO)
1. Inform the area supervisor and the machine operators.
2. Switch off the equipment using the normal stop.
3. Isolate all energy sources: electrical, pneumatic, hydraulic, stored mechanical energy.
4. Apply your personal padlock and tag to every isolation point. Every person working on the equipment applies their own lock.
5. Release stored energy and wait the discharge time stated in the machine manual.
6. Verify zero energy: try to start the machine and measure voltage.
7. After the work, remove only your own lock. A lock left by someone who has left the site may only be removed by the area manager after a documented check, with the EHS head informed.

## 5. Work permits
A work permit signed by the area supervisor and EHS is required for: hot work (welding, grinding, cutting outside the grinding bay), work at height above 1.8 meters, confined space entry, and electrical work on live equipment. Permits are valid for one shift. Hot work requires a fire watch for 30 minutes after the work ends. Electrical work on live equipment above 50 V additionally needs the approval of the plant electrical engineer.

## 6. Forklifts
Only employees holding a valid Helix forklift license (renewed every 3 years) may drive forklifts. Speed limit 10 km/h in the warehouse and 5 km/h in production halls. Pedestrians have priority on marked walkways. Contractors may not drive Helix forklifts.

## 7. Working hours and fatigue
No one may work more than 12 hours in a shift or more than 60 hours in a week, including overtime. Between two shifts there must be a rest period of at least 11 hours. Exceptions need the written approval of the plant manager and may not be used for more than 2 consecutive weeks.

## 8. Training
| Training | Who | Refresh |
|---|---|---|
| General safety induction | Everyone | Every 2 years |
| Fire extinguisher use | Everyone in production | Every year |
| First aid | 1 person per 25 employees per shift | Every 2 years |
| LOTO | Maintenance and Level 2 technicians | Every year |
| Forklift | Forklift drivers | Every 3 years |

## 9. Changes from Version 4
- The near-miss deadline is now 24 hours (was 48 hours).
- Hearing protection is required in the machining hall above 85 dB(A) (was 90 dB(A)).
- Contractor badges are valid for 12 months (were 6 months).
- The maximum working week is 60 hours (was 64 hours).

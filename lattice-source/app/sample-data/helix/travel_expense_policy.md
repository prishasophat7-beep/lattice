# Helix Precision - Travel and Expense Policy (FIN-07, 2026)

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets the rules for business travel, accommodation, daily allowances and the reimbursement of expenses. It applies to all employees. Expenses must be submitted in the Expense portal within 30 days of the end of the trip; claims submitted after 60 days are not reimbursed.

## 2. Employee grades
| Grade | Examples |
|---|---|
| G1 | Operators, technicians, clerks |
| G2 | Engineers, supervisors, specialists |
| G3 | Managers |
| G4 | Senior managers, plant managers |
| G5 | Directors and above |

## 3. Travel class

| Grade | Air (domestic) | Air (international, up to 6 hours) | Air (international, over 6 hours) | Train |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | Economy | Economy | Economy | AC 3-tier |
| G2 | Economy | Economy | Premium economy | AC 2-tier |
| G3 | Economy | Premium economy | Business | AC 2-tier |
| G4 | Economy | Business | Business | AC 1st class |
| G5 | Business | Business | Business | AC 1st class |

Flights must be booked through the travel desk at least 7 days before travel. Bookings made less than 7 days ahead need the approval of the employee's manager with a reason. The lowest logical fare within 2 hours of the preferred departure time must be chosen.

## 4. Accommodation limits per night (INR)

| Grade | Tier 1 city | Tier 2 city | Other |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | 4,000 | 3,000 | 2,500 |
| G2 | 6,000 | 4,500 | 3,500 |
| G3 | 8,000 | 6,000 | 4,500 |
| G4 | 12,000 | 8,000 | 6,000 |
| G5 | Actuals | Actuals | Actuals |

Tier 1 cities: Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune, Kolkata. Tier 2 cities: Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Coimbatore, Nagpur, Kochi, Indore and other state capitals.
Staying with friends or relatives instead of a hotel is allowed and is compensated with a flat INR 1,000 per night.

## 5. Daily allowance (per diem)
The daily allowance covers meals, local conveyance within the city and incidentals.

| Grade | Domestic (INR per day) | International (USD per day) |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | 800 | 50 |
| G2 | 1,200 | 70 |
| G3 | 1,600 | 90 |
| G4 | 2,000 | 110 |
| G5 | 2,500 | 130 |

Trips shorter than 8 hours get half the daily allowance. If meals are provided by the host or included in a conference fee, the allowance is reduced by 25 % for each main meal provided (breakfast, lunch, dinner). The allowance is not paid for days of personal leave taken during a trip.

## 6. Approvals

| Expense | Approval needed |
|---|---|
| Domestic trip | Manager |
| International trip | Manager and the department head (G4 or above) |
| Single expense above INR 25,000 | Department head |
| Single expense above INR 1,00,000 | Plant manager and finance controller |
| Accommodation above the grade limit | Department head, before the booking |
| Business-class air for a grade not entitled to it | Managing director |

## 7. Receipts
Original receipts or tax invoices are required for every expense above INR 500, except the daily allowance. Lost receipts: a self-declaration is accepted up to INR 2,000 per trip; above that the expense is not reimbursed. Receipts must show the GST number of the supplier where applicable.

## 8. Not reimbursed
Alcohol, minibar, laundry for trips shorter than 4 nights, personal entertainment, traffic fines, upgrades bought by the employee, travel insurance bought separately (the company policy covers all business trips), and expenses for accompanying family members.

## 9. Advances
An advance of up to 80 % of the estimated trip cost may be requested for trips longer than 3 days or for international trips. The advance must be settled within 15 days of return; unsettled advances are recovered from the next salary.

## 10. Mileage
Use of a personal car for business travel is reimbursed at INR 12 per km, and a personal two-wheeler at INR 5 per km, for distances above 20 km per day. Tolls and parking are reimbursed at actuals with receipts.

# Supply Agreement - Arvind Metals Pvt. Ltd. (with Amendment 1)

## Part 1: Original agreement (signed 10 January 2025)

### 1.1 Scope
Arvind Metals supplies aluminium bar stock (grades 6061-T6 and 7075-T6) and steel bar (EN8, EN24) to the Helix Precision plants in Pune and Chennai.

### 1.2 Term
The agreement runs from 1 February 2025 to 31 January 2027 and renews automatically for one year unless either party gives 90 days' written notice.

### 1.3 Prices
Prices are fixed per quarter and linked to the LME aluminium price of the last month of the previous quarter. A change of more than 5 % in the LME price allows either party to request a price review.

### 1.4 Delivery
Delivery within 10 working days of the purchase order. On-time delivery target: 95 % of orders per quarter.

### 1.5 Payment terms
Payment within 60 days of the invoice date.

### 1.6 Late delivery penalty
For each full week of delay the supplier pays a penalty of 1 % of the value of the delayed order, up to a maximum of 5 % of the order value.

### 1.7 Quality
Each delivery must come with a mill test certificate. Material that fails incoming inspection is returned at the supplier's cost and must be replaced within 5 working days.

### 1.8 Termination
Helix may terminate the agreement with 30 days' notice if on-time delivery is below 85 % for two consecutive quarters.

## Part 2: Amendment 1 (signed 15 March 2026, effective 1 April 2026)
The following clauses replace the corresponding clauses of the original agreement. All other clauses remain unchanged.

### 2.1 Delivery (replaces 1.4)
Delivery within 7 working days of the purchase order for 6061-T6 and within 10 working days for all other grades. On-time delivery target: 97 % of orders per quarter.

### 2.2 Payment terms (replaces 1.5)
Payment within 45 days of the invoice date. An early-payment discount of 1.5 % applies to invoices paid within 15 days.

### 2.3 Late delivery penalty (replaces 1.6)
For each full week of delay the supplier pays a penalty of 1.5 % of the value of the delayed order, up to a maximum of 7.5 % of the order value. No penalty applies to delays caused by force majeure notified in writing within 48 hours.

### 2.4 Safety stock (new clause)
Arvind Metals keeps a safety stock of at least 4 tonnes of 6061-T6 bar at its Pune warehouse reserved for Helix.

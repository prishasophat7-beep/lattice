# 3 What Is Not Covered

_Manual p. 1_

- Damage caused by accidents, liquid, fire, lightning or power surges.
- Products that have been opened, modified or repaired by anyone other than Skylink.
- Use of third-party or custom firmware.
- Cosmetic damage and normal wear and tear.
- Products with a removed or altered serial number.
- Loss of data or configuration settings.

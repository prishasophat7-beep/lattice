# 4.1 No Internet

_Manual p. 2_

1. Confirm whether all devices or only one device is affected.
2. Check the INTERNET LED. If off, reseat the WAN cable at both ends.
3. Power-cycle the modem first, wait 2 minutes, then power-cycle the router.
4. If the INTERNET LED stays amber, ask the customer to contact their ISP to confirm there is no outage.
5. If still unresolved, collect WAN logs (Admin Console > Diagnostics > Export) and escalate.

# 5 Escalation

_Manual p. 3_

Escalate only after completing the relevant steps above and attaching logs. Tickets escalated without logs will be returned.

| Condition | Escalate to | Target response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No internet after full diagnostic flow | Tier 2 Network | 4 business hours |
| POWER LED amber (hardware fault) | RMA team | 1 business day |
| E-217 after manual upload | Tier 2 Firmware | 4 business hours |
| Suspected security issue (unknown admin login) | Security on-call | 1 hour, 24/7 |
| More than 20 tickets with same symptom in 1 hour | Incident manager | 15 minutes |

# Customer Feedback

_Manual pages not recorded_

We analyzed 3,904 CSAT survey comments. Positive comments most often mention agent friendliness and quick fixes. Negative comments cluster around the firmware update issue and having to explain the problem again after escalation.

- "Agent fixed my internet in five minutes and explained what went wrong."
- "Third time contacting you about the same update error."
- "I had to repeat everything when I was transferred to Tier 2."

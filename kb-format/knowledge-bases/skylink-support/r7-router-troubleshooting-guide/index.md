# R7 router troubleshooting guide

Source file: `r7_router_troubleshooting_guide.pdf` · 3 pages

Explains that this guide is for Tier 1 agents handling Skylink R7 router tickets and lists what to collect before troubleshooting. Shows the front panel LEDs and explains what each color and blink pattern means. Presents the no-internet decision flowchart that routes agents from power checks to reboot to Tier 2 escalation. Covers the three most frequent issue types: no internet, slow Wi-Fi and failed firmware updates.

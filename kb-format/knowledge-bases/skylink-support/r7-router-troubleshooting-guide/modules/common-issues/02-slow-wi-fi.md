# 4.2 Slow Wi-Fi

_Manual p. 2_

- Ask the customer to run a speed test next to the router, then in the problem room.
- Recommend the 5 GHz network for devices within two rooms of the router.
- Move the router off the floor and away from microwaves and fish tanks.
- Enable Auto Channel (Wireless > Advanced) to avoid congested channels.
- Confirm firmware is 2.9.1 or later, which fixes a 5 GHz throughput issue.

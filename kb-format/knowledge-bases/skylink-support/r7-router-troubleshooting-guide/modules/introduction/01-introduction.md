# 1 Introduction

_Manual p. 1_

This guide helps Tier 1 support agents resolve the most common Skylink R7 router issues on the first contact. Follow the sections in order unless the customer's symptom clearly matches a specific issue in Section 4.

Before starting, collect the router serial number (on the label under the device), the firmware version (Admin Console > System) and the customer's ISP. Log all three in the ticket.

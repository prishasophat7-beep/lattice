# Q3 2026 support review

Source file: `q3_2026_support_review.docx`

Summarizes Q3: 12,480 tickets resolved, CSAT up to 91.3%, faster first response, but a growing backlog after the R7 2.9.0 rollout. Shows the KPI dashboard and a table comparing Q2 and Q3 values against targets. Describes weekly ticket volume through Q3, with a peak of 1,260 tickets in week 34 due to the R7 2.9.0 rollout. Ranks the five largest contact reasons, led by no-internet issues and firmware update failures.

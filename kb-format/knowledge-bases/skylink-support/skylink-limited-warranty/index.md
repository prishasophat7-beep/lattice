# Skylink limited warranty

Source file: `skylink_limited_warranty.pdf` · 2 pages

States that Skylink warrants hardware against defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. Gives the warranty period by product line, from 1 year for accessories to 3 years for routers. Lists exclusions such as accidental damage, unauthorized modification, normal wear and third-party firmware. Explains the steps to file a warranty claim through the support portal and ship the product for RMA.

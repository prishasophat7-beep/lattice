# 1 What This Warranty Covers

_Manual p. 1_

Skylink Networks Inc. ("Skylink") warrants that its hardware products will be free from defects in materials and workmanship under normal use during the warranty period. If a defect arises, Skylink will, at its option, repair the product, replace it with a new or refurbished product of equal or better functionality, or refund the purchase price.

This warranty applies only to the original purchaser and only to products purchased from Skylink or an authorized reseller. Proof of purchase is required.

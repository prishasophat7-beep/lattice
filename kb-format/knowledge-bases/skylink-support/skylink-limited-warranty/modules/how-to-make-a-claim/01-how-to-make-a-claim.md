# 4 How to Make a Claim

_Manual p. 1_

1. Contact Skylink Support at support.skylink.example or 1-800-555-0142.
2. Complete troubleshooting with an agent to confirm a hardware defect.
3. Receive a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number by email.
4. Ship the product with the RMA number to the address provided. Skylink pays return shipping for valid claims.
5. Replacement products ship within 5 business days of receipt.

Customers with Skylink Care+ receive advance replacement: a replacement ships before the defective unit is returned.

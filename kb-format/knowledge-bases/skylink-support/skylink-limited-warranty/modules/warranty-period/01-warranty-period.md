# 2 Warranty Period

_Manual p. 1_

| R7 and R9 routers | 3 years | Skylink Care+ extends to 5 years |
| M3 mesh nodes | 2 years | Skylink Care+ extends to 4 years |
| Power adapters and cables | 1 year | Not available |
| Refurbished products | 1 year | Not available |

# Multi-Factor Authentication

_Manual pages not recorded_

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory. You can use Okta Verify on your phone, or a YubiKey that IT provides on request. SMS codes are not allowed.

1. Install Okta Verify from the App Store or Google Play.
2. On the MFA setup screen, choose Okta Verify and scan the QR code.
3. Approve the test push notification.
4. Optional: register a YubiKey as a backup factor.

# Employee handbook 2026

Source file: `employee_handbook_2026.pdf` · 3 pages

Welcomes new employees to Acme Corp and explains the purpose of the handbook. Shows the leadership team org chart and headcount by department. Covers core hours, paid time off by tenure, other leave types and remote work rules. Describes pay schedule, reviews and the main benefits including health, 401(k) match and learning budget. Summarizes expected behavior, anti-harassment rules, conflicts of interest and how to report concerns.

# 1 Welcome

_Manual p. 1_

Welcome to Acme Corp! Whether you joined this week or ten years ago, this handbook explains how we work together, what you can expect from us and what we expect from you. It does not replace your employment agreement; where the two differ, your agreement applies.

Acme builds workflow software used by more than 4,000 companies. We have about 300 employees across offices in Austin, Toronto and Lisbon, plus a growing remote team.

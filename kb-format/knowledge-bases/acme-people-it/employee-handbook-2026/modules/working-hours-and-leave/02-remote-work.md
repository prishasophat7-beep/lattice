# 3.1 Remote Work

_Manual p. 2_

Employees who live within 50 km of an office work from the office at least two days per week (Tuesday and Thursday are team days). Fully remote roles are approved by the department head. All employees receive a one-time **USD 750 home office stipend** and USD 50 per month for internet costs.

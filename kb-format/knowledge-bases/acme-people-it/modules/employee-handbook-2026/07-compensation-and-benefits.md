# 4 Compensation and Benefits

_Manual pp. 2–3_

Employees are paid semi-monthly on the 15th and last business day of each month. Compensation is reviewed once a year in March, based on performance and market data.

| Benefit | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Health, dental, vision | Acme pays 90% of premiums for employees and 75% for dependents |
| 401(k) | 100% match on the first 4% of salary, vesting immediately |
| Learning budget | USD 1,500 per year for courses, books and conferences |
| Wellness | USD 60 per month for gym, apps or classes |
| Life and disability | 2x salary life insurance; short and long-term disability |
| Equity | Stock options for all full-time employees, 4-year vesting with 1-year cliff |

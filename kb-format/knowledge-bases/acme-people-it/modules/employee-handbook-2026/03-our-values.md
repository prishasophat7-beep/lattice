# 1.1 Our Values

_Manual p. 1_

- **Customers first** - we start with the customer's problem, not our solution.
- **Own it** - if you see a problem, you own it until it is solved or handed off clearly.
- **Be candid** - give and ask for direct feedback, kindly.
- **Sweat the craft** - quality is everyone's job.

# 5 Code of Conduct

_Manual p. 3_

We expect everyone to treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated.

- Disclose any potential conflict of interest to your manager and HR.
- Do not accept gifts worth more than USD 100 from customers or vendors.
- Protect confidential information, including customer data and unreleased product plans.
- Report concerns to your manager, HR, or anonymously via the EthicsLine (ethics.acme-corp.com).

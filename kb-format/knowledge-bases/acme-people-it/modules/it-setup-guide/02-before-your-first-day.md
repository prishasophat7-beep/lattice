# Before Your First Day

_Manual pages not recorded_

Your laptop (MacBook Pro 14" or Dell XPS 15, depending on your role) ships to your home address 5 business days before your start date. You will receive an email at your personal address with your Acme username and a one-time activation link, valid for 72 hours.

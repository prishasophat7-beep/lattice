# Reporting Suspicious Messages

_Manual pages not recorded_

1. Do not click links, open attachments or reply.
2. Click the **Report Phish** button in Gmail (or forward to phish@acme-corp.com).
3. If you already clicked or entered your password, call the IT help desk at ext. 4357 immediately.
4. Security will reply within 1 hour to let you know if the message was safe.

You will never get in trouble for reporting a message, even if it turns out to be legitimate.

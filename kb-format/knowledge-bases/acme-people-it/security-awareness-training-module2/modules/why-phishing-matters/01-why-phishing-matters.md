# Why Phishing Matters

_Manual pages not recorded_

Phishing is the most common way attackers get into companies. In 2025, 71% of the security incidents investigated at Acme started with a phishing email or message. Attackers do not only target executives: anyone with access to email, Slack or customer data is a target.

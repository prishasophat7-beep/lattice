# 4.2 Environment

_Manual pages not recorded_

Ambient temperature 10 - 35 °C, relative humidity below 75 % (non-condensing). For accuracy work (tolerances below 0.01 mm) the room temperature must be held within +/- 1 °C of 20 °C.

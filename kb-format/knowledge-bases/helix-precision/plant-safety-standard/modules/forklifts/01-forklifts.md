# 6. Forklifts

_Manual pages not recorded_

Only employees holding a valid Helix forklift license (renewed every 3 years) may drive forklifts. Speed limit 10 km/h in the warehouse and 5 km/h in production halls. Pedestrians have priority on marked walkways. Contractors may not drive Helix forklifts.

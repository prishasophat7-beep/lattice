# Leave policy

Source file: `leave_policy.pdf`

Acme Technologies' HR leave policy (HR-POL-007 v3.1) defining entitlements for privilege, casual/sick, maternity/paternity, marriage, compassionate, award and medical checkup leave. It also sets out the HRMS-based approval workflow, the annual holiday list and an annexure of abbreviations.

| Module | Pages | Summary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [LEAVE POLICY](modules/leave-policy/01-leave-policy.md) | pages not recorded | Title block of Acme Technologies' leave policy, document HR-POL-007 version 3.1, effective 1 April 2026. |
| [Purpose and Scope:](modules/purpose-and-scope/01-purpose-and-scope.md) | pages not recorded | Defines the purpose of providing employees with structured time off and states the policy applies to all permanent and probationary employees in India. |
| [Policy:](modules/policy/01-policy.md) | pages not recorded | States that the leave year runs January-December, leave accrues monthly, and all leave must be applied for through the HRMS portal. |
| [A. Privilege Leave & Casual / Sick Leave](modules/a-privilege-leave-and-casual-sick-leave/01-a-privilege-leave-and-casual-sick-leave.md) | pages not recorded | Employees receive 18 days privilege leave (max 45 carried forward) and 12 days casual/sick leave (not carried forward); sick leave over 2 days needs a medical certificate. |
| [B. Maternity / Paternity Leave](modules/b-maternity-paternity-leave/01-b-maternity-paternity-leave.md) | pages not recorded | Female employees receive 26 weeks paid maternity leave for the first two children (12 weeks thereafter); fathers get 10 working days paternity leave within 3 months of birth. |
| [C. Marriage And Compassionate Leave](modules/c-marriage-and-compassionate-leave/01-c-marriage-and-compassionate-leave.md) | pages not recorded | Grants 5 working days marriage leave once during employment and 5 days compassionate leave on the death of an immediate family member. |
| [D. Award Leave](modules/d-award-leave/01-d-award-leave.md) | pages not recorded | Employees recognised in the annual awards or completing 5/10 years of service receive 2-5 additional days of award leave to be used within 6 months. |
| [E. Official Medical Checkup Leave](modules/e-official-medical-checkup-leave/01-e-official-medical-checkup-leave.md) | pages not recorded | Employees aged 35 and above get one day of paid leave per year for the company-sponsored annual health checkup. |
| [Leave Approval Procedure](modules/leave-approval-procedure/01-leave-approval-procedure.md) | pages not recorded | Leave is applied on HRMS, reviewed by the reporting manager within 2 working days, escalated to the HOD if over 5 days, then recorded by HR (see figure_000.png). |
| [HOLIDAYS](modules/holidays/01-holidays.md) | pages not recorded | The company observes 10 paid public holidays per year (8 fixed national/festival holidays plus 2 optional holidays chosen by the employee); the list is published each December. |
| [ANNEXURE](modules/annexure/01-annexure.md) | pages not recorded | Contains the leave summary table and abbreviations (PL, CL/SL, LWP, HOD, HRMS) used throughout the policy. |

# E. Official Medical Checkup Leave

_Manual pages not recorded_

Employees aged 35 years and above are entitled to one day of paid leave per calendar year to undergo the company-sponsored annual health checkup at an empanelled hospital. The checkup report receipt must be uploaded to the HRMS portal within 7 days.

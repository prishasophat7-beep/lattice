# Policy:

_Manual pages not recorded_

The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Leave entitlements accrue monthly and are pro-rated for employees joining mid-year. All leave must be applied for and approved through the HRMS portal; leave taken without approval is treated as Leave Without Pay (LWP).

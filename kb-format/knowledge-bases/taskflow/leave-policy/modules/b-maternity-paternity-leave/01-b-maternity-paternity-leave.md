# B. Maternity / Paternity Leave

_Manual pages not recorded_

- **Maternity leave:** 26 weeks of paid leave for the first two children, and 12 weeks for subsequent children, in line with the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961. Up to 8 weeks may be taken before the expected delivery date.
- **Adoption:** 12 weeks for a mother adopting a child below 3 months of age.
- **Miscarriage:** 6 weeks of paid leave.
- **Paternity leave:** 10 working days, to be availed within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption.
- Employees must have completed 80 days of service in the preceding 12 months to be eligible.

# D. Award Leave

_Manual pages not recorded_

| Occasion | Award Leave |
|---|---|
| Employee of the Year | 5 days |
| Quarterly Star Performer | 2 days |
| 5 years of service | 3 days |
| 10 years of service | 5 days |

Award leave must be used within 6 months of being granted and cannot be encashed or carried forward.

# Purpose and Scope:

_Manual pages not recorded_

The purpose of this policy is to give employees adequate time off for rest, personal matters, health and family events while ensuring business continuity. This policy applies to all permanent and probationary employees on the company's payroll in India. Consultants, interns and contract staff are governed by their respective agreements.

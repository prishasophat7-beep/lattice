# A. Privilege Leave & Casual / Sick Leave

_Manual pages not recorded_

| Leave Type | Annual Entitlement | Accrual | Carry Forward | Encashment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Privilege Leave (PL) | 18 days | 1.5 days/month | Up to 45 days | Up to 15 days at year-end |
| Casual / Sick Leave (CL/SL) | 12 days | 1 day/month | Not permitted | Not permitted |

- PL must be applied for at least 7 days in advance for absences longer than 3 days.
- PL can be availed only after completing the probation period.
- CL/SL of more than 2 consecutive days requires a medical certificate from a registered practitioner.
- CL cannot be combined with PL.

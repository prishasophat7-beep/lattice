# HOLIDAYS

_Manual pages not recorded_

The company observes 10 paid holidays per calendar year: 8 fixed holidays and 2 optional (restricted) holidays chosen by the employee from a published list. Fixed holidays for 2026 include Republic Day (26 Jan), Holi (4 Mar), Good Friday (3 Apr), May Day (1 May), Independence Day (15 Aug), Gandhi Jayanti (2 Oct), Diwali (8 Nov) and Christmas (25 Dec). Holidays falling within a period of PL are not counted as leave.

# C. Marriage And Compassionate Leave

_Manual pages not recorded_

- **Marriage leave:** 5 working days, available once during employment, to be taken within 1 month of the wedding date. Proof (invitation or certificate) is required.
- **Compassionate leave:** 5 working days on the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling, parent-in-law). Can be applied for retrospectively.

# 26.4 Tighten the lubrication unit coupling

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose fuse on the lubrication unit causes vibration and must be replaceed within 11 hours. If the lubrication unit shows wear above 10 %, lubricate the valve before the next shift. If the lubrication unit shows wear above 10 %, lubricate the relay before the next shift. If the lubrication unit shows wear above 70 %, clean the fuse before the next shift. Use only Helix-approved gaskets on the lubrication unit; others void the service agreement. If the lubrication unit shows wear above 80 %, check the coupling before the next shift.

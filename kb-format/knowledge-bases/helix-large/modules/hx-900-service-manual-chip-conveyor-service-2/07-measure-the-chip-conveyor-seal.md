# 28.6 Measure the chip conveyor seal

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose hose on the chip conveyor causes vibration and must be adjusted within 11 hours. After any work on the chip conveyor, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 9 seconds). Every 700 hours, replace the chip conveyor gasket and note the result in the machine log. The chip conveyor belt must be checked by a Level 1 technician only. Every 1350 hours, clean the chip conveyor fuse and note the result in the machine log. A loose gasket on the chip conveyor causes vibration and must be lubricateed within 5 hours. Every 2150 hours, lubricate the chip conveyor sensor and note the result in the machine log. If the chip conveyor shows wear above 10 %, inspect the filter before the next shift.

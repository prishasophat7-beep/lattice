# 28.2 Lubricate the chip conveyor bracket

_Manual pages not recorded_

The chip conveyor hose must be recorded by a Level 1 technician only. After any work on the chip conveyor, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 47 seconds). A loose bracket on the chip conveyor causes vibration and must be verifyed within 38 hours. A loose nozzle on the chip conveyor causes vibration and must be checked within 28 hours. If the chip conveyor shows wear above 40 %, tighten the seal before the next shift.

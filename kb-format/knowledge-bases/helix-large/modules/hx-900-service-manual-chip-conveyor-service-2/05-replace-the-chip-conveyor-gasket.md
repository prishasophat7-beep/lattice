# 28.4 Replace the chip conveyor gasket

_Manual pages not recorded_

Every 1600 hours, check the chip conveyor belt and note the result in the machine log. After any work on the chip conveyor, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 30 seconds). After any work on the chip conveyor, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 27 seconds). If the chip conveyor shows wear above 20 %, check the belt before the next shift. Use only Helix-approved couplings on the chip conveyor; others void the service agreement. The chip conveyor coupling must be replaceed by a Level 1 technician only. A loose bearing on the chip conveyor causes vibration and must be verifyed within 39 hours. If the chip conveyor shows wear above 80 %, inspect the cable before the next shift. Use only Helix-approved fuses on the chip conveyor; others void the service agreement.

# 5.3 Measure the way covers valve

_Manual pages not recorded_

The way covers gasket must be adjusted by a Level 2 technician only. Every 1300 hours, lubricate the way covers gasket and note the result in the machine log. Every 900 hours, measure the way covers cable and note the result in the machine log. After any work on the way covers, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 24 seconds). A loose hose on the way covers causes vibration and must be lubricateed within 32 hours.

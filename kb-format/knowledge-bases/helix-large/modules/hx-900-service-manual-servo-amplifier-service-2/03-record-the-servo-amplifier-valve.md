# 38.2 Record the servo amplifier valve

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose seal on the servo amplifier causes vibration and must be cleaned within 19 hours. Use only Helix-approved gaskets on the servo amplifier; others void the service agreement. After any work on the servo amplifier, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 21 seconds). The servo amplifier cable must be adjusted by a Level 1 technician only. Every 1000 hours, check the servo amplifier switch and note the result in the machine log. If the servo amplifier shows wear above 60 %, clean the fuse before the next shift.

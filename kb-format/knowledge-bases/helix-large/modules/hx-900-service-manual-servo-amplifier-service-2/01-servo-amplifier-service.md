# 38. Servo Amplifier service

_Manual pages not recorded_

Use only Helix-approved filters on the servo amplifier; others void the service agreement. After any work on the servo amplifier, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 14 seconds). Use only Helix-approved brackets on the servo amplifier; others void the service agreement.

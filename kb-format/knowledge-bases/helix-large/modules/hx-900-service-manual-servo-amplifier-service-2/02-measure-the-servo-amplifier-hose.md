# 38.1 Measure the servo amplifier hose

_Manual pages not recorded_

The servo amplifier filter must be checked by a Level 1 technician only. A loose bearing on the servo amplifier causes vibration and must be inspected within 48 hours. If the servo amplifier shows wear above 20 %, check the cable before the next shift. If the servo amplifier shows wear above 10 %, inspect the bracket before the next shift. If the servo amplifier shows wear above 20 %, adjust the bearing before the next shift. If the servo amplifier shows wear above 80 %, adjust the coupling before the next shift. After any work on the servo amplifier, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 42 seconds). The servo amplifier nozzle must be recorded by a Level 2 technician only.

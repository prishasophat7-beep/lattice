# 27.5 Clean the hydraulic clamp relay

_Manual pages not recorded_

Use only Helix-approved cables on the hydraulic clamp; others void the service agreement. If the hydraulic clamp shows wear above 70 %, clean the fuse before the next shift. A loose fuse on the hydraulic clamp causes vibration and must be measureed within 15 hours. Use only Helix-approved belts on the hydraulic clamp; others void the service agreement. The hydraulic clamp valve must be verifyed by a Level 1 technician only.

# 27. Hydraulic Clamp maintenance

_Manual pages not recorded_

Every 2000 hours, record the hydraulic clamp filter and note the result in the machine log. The hydraulic clamp gasket must be measureed by a Level 2 technician only. After any work on the hydraulic clamp, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 14 seconds).

# 27.6 Verify the hydraulic clamp coupling

_Manual pages not recorded_

If the hydraulic clamp shows wear above 70 %, inspect the sensor before the next shift. Every 1250 hours, inspect the hydraulic clamp coupling and note the result in the machine log. If the hydraulic clamp shows wear above 30 %, inspect the seal before the next shift. A loose seal on the hydraulic clamp causes vibration and must be inspected within 36 hours. If the hydraulic clamp shows wear above 60 %, lubricate the belt before the next shift. Every 1800 hours, record the hydraulic clamp valve and note the result in the machine log. The hydraulic clamp coupling must be measureed by a Level 2 technician only. Use only Helix-approved sensors on the hydraulic clamp; others void the service agreement.

# 40.1 Inspect the mist collector nozzle

_Manual pages not recorded_

The mist collector fuse must be lubricateed by a Level 1 technician only. Use only Helix-approved nozzles on the mist collector; others void the service agreement. The mist collector relay must be replaceed by a Level 2 technician only. If the mist collector shows wear above 90 %, tighten the nozzle before the next shift. The mist collector seal must be adjusted by a Level 1 technician only.

# 40.5 Measure the mist collector valve

_Manual pages not recorded_

The mist collector cable must be adjusted by a Level 1 technician only. Every 1450 hours, inspect the mist collector filter and note the result in the machine log. After any work on the mist collector, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 17 seconds). A loose filter on the mist collector causes vibration and must be inspected within 18 hours. After any work on the mist collector, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 46 seconds).

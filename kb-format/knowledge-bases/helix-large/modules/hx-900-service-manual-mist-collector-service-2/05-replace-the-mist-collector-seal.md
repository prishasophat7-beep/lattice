# 40.4 Replace the mist collector seal

_Manual pages not recorded_

If the mist collector shows wear above 40 %, inspect the gasket before the next shift. The mist collector filter must be inspected by a Level 2 technician only. If the mist collector shows wear above 20 %, lubricate the bearing before the next shift. The mist collector coupling must be checked by a Level 1 technician only. After any work on the mist collector, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 16 seconds). Every 1250 hours, adjust the mist collector coupling and note the result in the machine log. Use only Helix-approved nozzles on the mist collector; others void the service agreement.

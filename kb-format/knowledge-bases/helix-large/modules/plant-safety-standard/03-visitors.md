# 2.2 Visitors

_Manual pages not recorded_

Visitors must be escorted at all times in production areas and wear safety shoes, safety glasses and a visitor vest. Visitors may not enter the heat treatment area or the paint shop. Visitors who are not wearing safety shoes may only use the marked visitor walkway.

# 8.1 Replace the chip conveyor belt

_Manual pages not recorded_

If the chip conveyor shows wear above 40 %, verify the filter before the next shift. After any work on the chip conveyor, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 15 seconds). The chip conveyor filter must be lubricateed by a Level 2 technician only. After any work on the chip conveyor, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 25 seconds). The chip conveyor cable must be verifyed by a Level 1 technician only. Use only Helix-approved relays on the chip conveyor; others void the service agreement.

# 8.2 Replace the chip conveyor sensor

_Manual pages not recorded_

Every 1000 hours, measure the chip conveyor bearing and note the result in the machine log. A loose coupling on the chip conveyor causes vibration and must be cleaned within 43 hours. The chip conveyor belt must be recorded by a Level 1 technician only. Use only Helix-approved couplings on the chip conveyor; others void the service agreement. Use only Helix-approved hoses on the chip conveyor; others void the service agreement.

# 8.3 Check the chip conveyor gasket

_Manual pages not recorded_

The chip conveyor coupling must be adjusted by a Level 2 technician only. After any work on the chip conveyor, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 34 seconds). If the chip conveyor shows wear above 70 %, adjust the belt before the next shift. Every 2200 hours, replace the chip conveyor fuse and note the result in the machine log. The chip conveyor filter must be adjusted by a Level 1 technician only. After any work on the chip conveyor, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 15 seconds).

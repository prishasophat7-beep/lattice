# 6. Lubrication Unit service

_Manual pages not recorded_

If the lubrication unit shows wear above 50 %, tighten the belt before the next shift. A loose coupling on the lubrication unit causes vibration and must be inspected within 12 hours. After any work on the lubrication unit, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 6 seconds).

# 20. Mist Collector service

_Manual pages not recorded_

If the mist collector shows wear above 30 %, lubricate the coupling before the next shift. A loose switch on the mist collector causes vibration and must be tightened within 10 hours. If the mist collector shows wear above 90 %, verify the bearing before the next shift.

# 20.1 Clean the mist collector relay

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose valve on the mist collector causes vibration and must be recorded within 32 hours. A loose nozzle on the mist collector causes vibration and must be lubricateed within 27 hours. Use only Helix-approved sensors on the mist collector; others void the service agreement. If the mist collector shows wear above 20 %, lubricate the relay before the next shift. Use only Helix-approved cables on the mist collector; others void the service agreement.

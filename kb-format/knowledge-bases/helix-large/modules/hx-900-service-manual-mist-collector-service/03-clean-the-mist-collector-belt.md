# 20.2 Clean the mist collector belt

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose bracket on the mist collector causes vibration and must be verifyed within 48 hours. The mist collector seal must be replaceed by a Level 1 technician only. After any work on the mist collector, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 44 seconds). Every 2000 hours, adjust the mist collector gasket and note the result in the machine log. If the mist collector shows wear above 30 %, check the gasket before the next shift. The mist collector sensor must be adjusted by a Level 1 technician only.

# 20.3 Tighten the mist collector filter

_Manual pages not recorded_

Every 1150 hours, inspect the mist collector belt and note the result in the machine log. The mist collector belt must be adjusted by a Level 2 technician only. The mist collector filter must be lubricateed by a Level 2 technician only. If the mist collector shows wear above 30 %, verify the bracket before the next shift. If the mist collector shows wear above 50 %, lubricate the hose before the next shift. After any work on the mist collector, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 14 seconds).

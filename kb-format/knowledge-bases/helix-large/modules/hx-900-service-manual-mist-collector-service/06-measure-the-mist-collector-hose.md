# 20.5 Measure the mist collector hose

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose belt on the mist collector causes vibration and must be recorded within 13 hours. If the mist collector shows wear above 10 %, measure the bearing before the next shift. The mist collector coupling must be checked by a Level 1 technician only. The mist collector hose must be adjusted by a Level 1 technician only. The mist collector nozzle must be checked by a Level 2 technician only. If the mist collector shows wear above 60 %, record the valve before the next shift.

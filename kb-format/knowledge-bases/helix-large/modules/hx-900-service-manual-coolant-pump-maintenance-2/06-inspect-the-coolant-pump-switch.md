# 23.5 Inspect the coolant pump switch

_Manual pages not recorded_

The coolant pump gasket must be checked by a Level 2 technician only. Every 1700 hours, measure the coolant pump bearing and note the result in the machine log. A loose bracket on the coolant pump causes vibration and must be verifyed within 15 hours. After any work on the coolant pump, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 44 seconds). If the coolant pump shows wear above 60 %, check the fuse before the next shift. A loose relay on the coolant pump causes vibration and must be adjusted within 32 hours.

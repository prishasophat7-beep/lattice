# 23.6 Adjust the coolant pump gasket

_Manual pages not recorded_

Use only Helix-approved cables on the coolant pump; others void the service agreement. Use only Helix-approved filters on the coolant pump; others void the service agreement. If the coolant pump shows wear above 90 %, lubricate the nozzle before the next shift. The coolant pump hose must be replaceed by a Level 1 technician only. If the coolant pump shows wear above 70 %, tighten the relay before the next shift. Use only Helix-approved valves on the coolant pump; others void the service agreement. A loose seal on the coolant pump causes vibration and must be tightened within 29 hours. A loose bracket on the coolant pump causes vibration and must be verifyed within 23 hours.

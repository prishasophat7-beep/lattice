# 17. Encoder maintenance

_Manual pages not recorded_

If the encoder shows wear above 60 %, record the seal before the next shift. After any work on the encoder, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 19 seconds). Use only Helix-approved cables on the encoder; others void the service agreement.

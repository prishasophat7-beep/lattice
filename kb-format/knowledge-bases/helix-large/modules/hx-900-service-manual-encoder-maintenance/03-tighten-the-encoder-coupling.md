# 17.2 Tighten the encoder coupling

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose relay on the encoder causes vibration and must be lubricateed within 27 hours. The encoder bearing must be tightened by a Level 2 technician only. After any work on the encoder, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 23 seconds). A loose relay on the encoder causes vibration and must be measureed within 18 hours. Every 1900 hours, record the encoder hose and note the result in the machine log.

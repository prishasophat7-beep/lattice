# 39.6 Verify the chiller bearing

_Manual pages not recorded_

Use only Helix-approved belts on the chiller; others void the service agreement. A loose fuse on the chiller causes vibration and must be recorded within 32 hours. After any work on the chiller, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 32 seconds). The chiller gasket must be verifyed by a Level 1 technician only. The chiller valve must be measureed by a Level 2 technician only. Use only Helix-approved filters on the chiller; others void the service agreement. Use only Helix-approved sensors on the chiller; others void the service agreement. Every 450 hours, lubricate the chiller switch and note the result in the machine log.

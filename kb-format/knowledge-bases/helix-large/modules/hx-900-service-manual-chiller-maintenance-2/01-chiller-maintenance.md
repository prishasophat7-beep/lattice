# 39. Chiller maintenance

_Manual pages not recorded_

After any work on the chiller, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 34 seconds). If the chiller shows wear above 90 %, lubricate the bracket before the next shift. Use only Helix-approved seals on the chiller; others void the service agreement.

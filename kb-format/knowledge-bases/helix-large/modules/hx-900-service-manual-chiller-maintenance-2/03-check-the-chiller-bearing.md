# 39.2 Check the chiller bearing

_Manual pages not recorded_

Every 1700 hours, replace the chiller belt and note the result in the machine log. Use only Helix-approved valves on the chiller; others void the service agreement. A loose seal on the chiller causes vibration and must be checked within 37 hours. The chiller gasket must be tightened by a Level 2 technician only. Every 1400 hours, record the chiller bearing and note the result in the machine log. Use only Helix-approved bearings on the chiller; others void the service agreement. Use only Helix-approved belts on the chiller; others void the service agreement. Every 2100 hours, measure the chiller belt and note the result in the machine log. The chiller bracket must be recorded by a Level 1 technician only.

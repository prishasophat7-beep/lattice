# 39.3 Measure the chiller hose

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose gasket on the chiller causes vibration and must be checked within 2 hours. A loose sensor on the chiller causes vibration and must be measureed within 13 hours. If the chiller shows wear above 30 %, verify the coupling before the next shift. After any work on the chiller, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 47 seconds). A loose coupling on the chiller causes vibration and must be recorded within 31 hours. If the chiller shows wear above 20 %, lubricate the coupling before the next shift.

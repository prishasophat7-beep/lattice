# 25.1 Measure the way covers relay

_Manual pages not recorded_

Every 900 hours, measure the way covers gasket and note the result in the machine log. The way covers belt must be recorded by a Level 1 technician only. If the way covers shows wear above 70 %, tighten the belt before the next shift. Use only Helix-approved cables on the way covers; others void the service agreement. The way covers sensor must be recorded by a Level 2 technician only.

# 25.6 Record the way covers sensor

_Manual pages not recorded_

If the way covers shows wear above 40 %, measure the belt before the next shift. If the way covers shows wear above 80 %, verify the coupling before the next shift. A loose bracket on the way covers causes vibration and must be recorded within 28 hours. After any work on the way covers, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 7 seconds). A loose belt on the way covers causes vibration and must be tightened within 6 hours. Every 200 hours, lubricate the way covers nozzle and note the result in the machine log.

# 25.2 Record the way covers relay

_Manual pages not recorded_

The way covers filter must be replaceed by a Level 2 technician only. Every 1550 hours, check the way covers cable and note the result in the machine log. A loose coupling on the way covers causes vibration and must be lubricateed within 12 hours. If the way covers shows wear above 80 %, inspect the nozzle before the next shift. After any work on the way covers, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 40 seconds). The way covers seal must be checked by a Level 2 technician only. Every 1000 hours, inspect the way covers nozzle and note the result in the machine log.

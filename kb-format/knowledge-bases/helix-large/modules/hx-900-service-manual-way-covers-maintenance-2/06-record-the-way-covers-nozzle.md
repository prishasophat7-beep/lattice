# 25.5 Record the way covers nozzle

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose belt on the way covers causes vibration and must be replaceed within 44 hours. After any work on the way covers, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 40 seconds). Every 1150 hours, tighten the way covers cable and note the result in the machine log. A loose seal on the way covers causes vibration and must be adjusted within 6 hours. After any work on the way covers, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 16 seconds). Every 600 hours, tighten the way covers bearing and note the result in the machine log. A loose valve on the way covers causes vibration and must be tightened within 15 hours. The way covers bearing must be adjusted by a Level 1 technician only. After any work on the way covers, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 23 seconds).

# 32.2 Record the pneumatic unit switch

_Manual pages not recorded_

The pneumatic unit fuse must be recorded by a Level 2 technician only. If the pneumatic unit shows wear above 30 %, replace the bearing before the next shift. The pneumatic unit sensor must be verifyed by a Level 1 technician only. If the pneumatic unit shows wear above 10 %, record the relay before the next shift. A loose coupling on the pneumatic unit causes vibration and must be tightened within 7 hours.

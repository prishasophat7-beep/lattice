# 13.4 Check the door interlock switch

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose belt on the door interlock causes vibration and must be checked within 18 hours. A loose cable on the door interlock causes vibration and must be adjusted within 14 hours. A loose cable on the door interlock causes vibration and must be replaceed within 34 hours. Use only Helix-approved brackets on the door interlock; others void the service agreement. The door interlock bracket must be adjusted by a Level 2 technician only. The door interlock filter must be adjusted by a Level 2 technician only. If the door interlock shows wear above 80 %, lubricate the relay before the next shift.

# 13.3 Measure the door interlock coupling

_Manual pages not recorded_

After any work on the door interlock, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 37 seconds). The door interlock valve must be checked by a Level 2 technician only. After any work on the door interlock, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 19 seconds). A loose hose on the door interlock causes vibration and must be replaceed within 20 hours. Every 900 hours, lubricate the door interlock belt and note the result in the machine log. If the door interlock shows wear above 10 %, check the hose before the next shift.

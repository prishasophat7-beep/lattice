# 3. Coolant Pump maintenance

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose seal on the coolant pump causes vibration and must be checked within 35 hours. The coolant pump seal must be adjusted by a Level 1 technician only. The coolant pump fuse must be inspected by a Level 1 technician only.

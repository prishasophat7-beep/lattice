# 3.4 Measure the coolant pump relay

_Manual pages not recorded_

Every 200 hours, adjust the coolant pump seal and note the result in the machine log. The coolant pump seal must be inspected by a Level 1 technician only. Use only Helix-approved seals on the coolant pump; others void the service agreement. If the coolant pump shows wear above 70 %, record the switch before the next shift. A loose bracket on the coolant pump causes vibration and must be tightened within 46 hours. Every 1700 hours, measure the coolant pump cable and note the result in the machine log.

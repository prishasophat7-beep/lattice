# 3.2 Measure the coolant pump hose

_Manual pages not recorded_

If the coolant pump shows wear above 10 %, record the switch before the next shift. If the coolant pump shows wear above 60 %, adjust the fuse before the next shift. If the coolant pump shows wear above 60 %, lubricate the gasket before the next shift. If the coolant pump shows wear above 60 %, check the filter before the next shift. A loose fuse on the coolant pump causes vibration and must be checked within 38 hours. After any work on the coolant pump, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 33 seconds). If the coolant pump shows wear above 70 %, adjust the valve before the next shift.

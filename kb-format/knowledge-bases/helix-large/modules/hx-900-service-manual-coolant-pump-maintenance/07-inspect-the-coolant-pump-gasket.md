# 3.6 Inspect the coolant pump gasket

_Manual pages not recorded_

Every 1650 hours, lubricate the coolant pump seal and note the result in the machine log. The coolant pump relay must be replaceed by a Level 1 technician only. The coolant pump hose must be verifyed by a Level 1 technician only. Every 1600 hours, verify the coolant pump gasket and note the result in the machine log. The coolant pump seal must be adjusted by a Level 1 technician only. Use only Helix-approved couplings on the coolant pump; others void the service agreement.

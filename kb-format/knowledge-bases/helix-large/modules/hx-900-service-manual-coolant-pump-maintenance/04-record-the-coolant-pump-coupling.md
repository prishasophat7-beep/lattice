# 3.3 Record the coolant pump coupling

_Manual pages not recorded_

Use only Helix-approved fuses on the coolant pump; others void the service agreement. If the coolant pump shows wear above 70 %, replace the belt before the next shift. The coolant pump switch must be recorded by a Level 1 technician only. Use only Helix-approved bearings on the coolant pump; others void the service agreement. If the coolant pump shows wear above 50 %, check the coupling before the next shift. Use only Helix-approved gaskets on the coolant pump; others void the service agreement.

# 34.6 Tighten the rotary table hose

_Manual pages not recorded_

Use only Helix-approved belts on the rotary table; others void the service agreement. Use only Helix-approved gaskets on the rotary table; others void the service agreement. A loose gasket on the rotary table causes vibration and must be checked within 28 hours. A loose bracket on the rotary table causes vibration and must be verifyed within 6 hours. A loose sensor on the rotary table causes vibration and must be checked within 32 hours.

# 1. Axis Drive maintenance

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose hose on the axis drive causes vibration and must be lubricateed within 25 hours. Use only Helix-approved brackets on the axis drive; others void the service agreement. A loose belt on the axis drive causes vibration and must be adjusted within 29 hours.

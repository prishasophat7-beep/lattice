# 1.4 Inspect the axis drive sensor

_Manual pages not recorded_

After any work on the axis drive, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 46 seconds). A loose fuse on the axis drive causes vibration and must be checked within 22 hours. If the axis drive shows wear above 80 %, adjust the fuse before the next shift. After any work on the axis drive, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 2 seconds). If the axis drive shows wear above 20 %, record the fuse before the next shift. Every 900 hours, tighten the axis drive valve and note the result in the machine log. A loose bracket on the axis drive causes vibration and must be tightened within 7 hours. A loose valve on the axis drive causes vibration and must be checked within 9 hours.

# 10.4 Replace the electrical cabinet seal

_Manual pages not recorded_

Every 2050 hours, adjust the electrical cabinet bracket and note the result in the machine log. If the electrical cabinet shows wear above 90 %, adjust the cable before the next shift. If the electrical cabinet shows wear above 40 %, adjust the valve before the next shift. A loose gasket on the electrical cabinet causes vibration and must be measureed within 17 hours. Use only Helix-approved relays on the electrical cabinet; others void the service agreement. Every 750 hours, tighten the electrical cabinet relay and note the result in the machine log. Use only Helix-approved cables on the electrical cabinet; others void the service agreement. Use only Helix-approved hoses on the electrical cabinet; others void the service agreement. A loose belt on the electrical cabinet causes vibration and must be recorded within 36 hours.

# 10.1 Measure the electrical cabinet bracket

_Manual pages not recorded_

If the electrical cabinet shows wear above 10 %, verify the cable before the next shift. Every 1550 hours, measure the electrical cabinet cable and note the result in the machine log. After any work on the electrical cabinet, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 31 seconds).

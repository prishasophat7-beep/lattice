# 7.5 Inspect the hydraulic clamp bearing

_Manual pages not recorded_

The hydraulic clamp seal must be replaceed by a Level 2 technician only. Every 1600 hours, lubricate the hydraulic clamp gasket and note the result in the machine log. The hydraulic clamp nozzle must be checked by a Level 2 technician only. The hydraulic clamp sensor must be replaceed by a Level 1 technician only. A loose bearing on the hydraulic clamp causes vibration and must be measureed within 44 hours. If the hydraulic clamp shows wear above 20 %, tighten the cable before the next shift. If the hydraulic clamp shows wear above 80 %, verify the bracket before the next shift. Every 1950 hours, check the hydraulic clamp bearing and note the result in the machine log.

# 7.2 Check the hydraulic clamp bracket

_Manual pages not recorded_

After any work on the hydraulic clamp, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 36 seconds). If the hydraulic clamp shows wear above 30 %, adjust the coupling before the next shift. The hydraulic clamp filter must be checked by a Level 2 technician only. The hydraulic clamp bearing must be tightened by a Level 2 technician only. The hydraulic clamp sensor must be replaceed by a Level 1 technician only. Every 900 hours, verify the hydraulic clamp fuse and note the result in the machine log. After any work on the hydraulic clamp, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 4 seconds). A loose hose on the hydraulic clamp causes vibration and must be checked within 10 hours. A loose cable on the hydraulic clamp causes vibration and must be measureed within 31 hours.

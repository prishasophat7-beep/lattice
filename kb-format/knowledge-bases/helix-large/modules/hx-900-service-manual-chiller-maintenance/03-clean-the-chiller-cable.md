# 19.2 Clean the chiller cable

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose coupling on the chiller causes vibration and must be measureed within 7 hours. Every 850 hours, measure the chiller valve and note the result in the machine log. Use only Helix-approved fuses on the chiller; others void the service agreement. The chiller filter must be adjusted by a Level 2 technician only. The chiller fuse must be checked by a Level 2 technician only. Every 2350 hours, inspect the chiller seal and note the result in the machine log. Use only Helix-approved nozzles on the chiller; others void the service agreement.

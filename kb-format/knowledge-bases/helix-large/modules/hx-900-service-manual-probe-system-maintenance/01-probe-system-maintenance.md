# 9. Probe System maintenance

_Manual pages not recorded_

If the probe system shows wear above 70 %, measure the fuse before the next shift. The probe system valve must be recorded by a Level 1 technician only. If the probe system shows wear above 10 %, replace the cable before the next shift.

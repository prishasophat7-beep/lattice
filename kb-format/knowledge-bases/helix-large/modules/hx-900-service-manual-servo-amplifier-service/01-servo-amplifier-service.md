# 18. Servo Amplifier service

_Manual pages not recorded_

Use only Helix-approved bearings on the servo amplifier; others void the service agreement. The servo amplifier switch must be recorded by a Level 1 technician only. The servo amplifier relay must be replaceed by a Level 2 technician only.

# 2.4 Clean the spindle cartridge coupling

_Manual pages not recorded_

After any work on the spindle cartridge, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 37 seconds). If the spindle cartridge shows wear above 40 %, record the sensor before the next shift. If the spindle cartridge shows wear above 20 %, record the coupling before the next shift. If the spindle cartridge shows wear above 30 %, adjust the nozzle before the next shift. If the spindle cartridge shows wear above 50 %, inspect the cable before the next shift. A loose seal on the spindle cartridge causes vibration and must be recorded within 40 hours.

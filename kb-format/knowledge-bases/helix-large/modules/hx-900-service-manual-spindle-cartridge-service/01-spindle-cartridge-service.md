# 2. Spindle Cartridge service

_Manual pages not recorded_

The spindle cartridge nozzle must be lubricateed by a Level 2 technician only. Every 1050 hours, verify the spindle cartridge bearing and note the result in the machine log. The spindle cartridge filter must be recorded by a Level 2 technician only.

# 37.6 Check the encoder coupling

_Manual pages not recorded_

The encoder filter must be checked by a Level 1 technician only. Use only Helix-approved bearings on the encoder; others void the service agreement. A loose seal on the encoder causes vibration and must be recorded within 2 hours. If the encoder shows wear above 50 %, replace the bracket before the next shift. The encoder sensor must be replaceed by a Level 1 technician only. Use only Helix-approved couplings on the encoder; others void the service agreement. After any work on the encoder, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 18 seconds). Use only Helix-approved hoses on the encoder; others void the service agreement.

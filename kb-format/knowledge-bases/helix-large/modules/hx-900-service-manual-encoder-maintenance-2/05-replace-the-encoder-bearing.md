# 37.4 Replace the encoder bearing

_Manual pages not recorded_

The encoder gasket must be replaceed by a Level 2 technician only. A loose filter on the encoder causes vibration and must be tightened within 17 hours. A loose gasket on the encoder causes vibration and must be lubricateed within 23 hours. A loose sensor on the encoder causes vibration and must be lubricateed within 20 hours. After any work on the encoder, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 4 seconds). The Z-axis motor coupling screws (M8, class 12.9) are tightened to 38 Nm in a cross pattern, then re-checked after 50 operating hours. If the encoder shows wear above 60 %, replace the belt before the next shift. A loose sensor on the encoder causes vibration and must be inspected within 42 hours. A loose seal on the encoder causes vibration and must be recorded within 5 hours.

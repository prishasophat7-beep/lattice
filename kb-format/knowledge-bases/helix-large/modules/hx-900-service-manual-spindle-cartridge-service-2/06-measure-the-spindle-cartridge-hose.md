# 22.5 Measure the spindle cartridge hose

_Manual pages not recorded_

Every 950 hours, replace the spindle cartridge fuse and note the result in the machine log. A loose bearing on the spindle cartridge causes vibration and must be measureed within 7 hours. If the spindle cartridge shows wear above 60 %, inspect the fuse before the next shift. Use only Helix-approved switchs on the spindle cartridge; others void the service agreement. Use only Helix-approved fuses on the spindle cartridge; others void the service agreement. The spindle cartridge relay must be adjusted by a Level 2 technician only.

# 22.4 Inspect the spindle cartridge bracket

_Manual pages not recorded_

The spindle cartridge fuse must be tightened by a Level 1 technician only. Use only Helix-approved bearings on the spindle cartridge; others void the service agreement. After any work on the spindle cartridge, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 5 seconds). Every 1800 hours, adjust the spindle cartridge cable and note the result in the machine log. A loose belt on the spindle cartridge causes vibration and must be checked within 8 hours. A loose fuse on the spindle cartridge causes vibration and must be lubricateed within 2 hours.

# 22.1 Inspect the spindle cartridge fuse

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose cable on the spindle cartridge causes vibration and must be lubricateed within 24 hours. Every 500 hours, tighten the spindle cartridge sensor and note the result in the machine log. Use only Helix-approved couplings on the spindle cartridge; others void the service agreement. After any work on the spindle cartridge, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 24 seconds). Every 1150 hours, record the spindle cartridge filter and note the result in the machine log. A loose filter on the spindle cartridge causes vibration and must be measureed within 25 hours. A loose filter on the spindle cartridge causes vibration and must be measureed within 35 hours.

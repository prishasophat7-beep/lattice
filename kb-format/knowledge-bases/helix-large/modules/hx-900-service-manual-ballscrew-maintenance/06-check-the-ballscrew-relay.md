# 15.5 Check the ballscrew relay

_Manual pages not recorded_

The ballscrew relay must be cleaned by a Level 2 technician only. A loose coupling on the ballscrew causes vibration and must be replaceed within 38 hours. Use only Helix-approved couplings on the ballscrew; others void the service agreement. A loose seal on the ballscrew causes vibration and must be checked within 34 hours. Every 2250 hours, adjust the ballscrew cable and note the result in the machine log. Every 150 hours, verify the ballscrew relay and note the result in the machine log. If the ballscrew shows wear above 80 %, check the nozzle before the next shift. Use only Helix-approved valves on the ballscrew; others void the service agreement. A loose sensor on the ballscrew causes vibration and must be cleaned within 36 hours.

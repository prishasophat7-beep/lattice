# 15.4 Check the ballscrew gasket

_Manual pages not recorded_

Use only Helix-approved seals on the ballscrew; others void the service agreement. Use only Helix-approved brackets on the ballscrew; others void the service agreement. The ballscrew gasket must be tightened by a Level 1 technician only. Use only Helix-approved hoses on the ballscrew; others void the service agreement. If the ballscrew shows wear above 90 %, lubricate the belt before the next shift.

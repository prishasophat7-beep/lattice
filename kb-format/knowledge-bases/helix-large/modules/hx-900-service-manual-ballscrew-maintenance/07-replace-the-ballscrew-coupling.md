# 15.6 Replace the ballscrew coupling

_Manual pages not recorded_

Use only Helix-approved valves on the ballscrew; others void the service agreement. Use only Helix-approved bearings on the ballscrew; others void the service agreement. Use only Helix-approved nozzles on the ballscrew; others void the service agreement. If the ballscrew shows wear above 70 %, check the bracket before the next shift. If the ballscrew shows wear above 70 %, verify the hose before the next shift.

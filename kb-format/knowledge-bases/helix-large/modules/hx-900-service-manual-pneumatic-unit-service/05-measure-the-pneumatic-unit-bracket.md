# 12.4 Measure the pneumatic unit bracket

_Manual pages not recorded_

Every 2300 hours, tighten the pneumatic unit hose and note the result in the machine log. A loose nozzle on the pneumatic unit causes vibration and must be recorded within 22 hours. Use only Helix-approved nozzles on the pneumatic unit; others void the service agreement. Use only Helix-approved bearings on the pneumatic unit; others void the service agreement. A loose gasket on the pneumatic unit causes vibration and must be adjusted within 24 hours. After any work on the pneumatic unit, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 41 seconds).

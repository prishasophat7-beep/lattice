# 12. Pneumatic Unit service

_Manual pages not recorded_

The pneumatic unit fuse must be cleaned by a Level 1 technician only. If the pneumatic unit shows wear above 70 %, record the hose before the next shift. After any work on the pneumatic unit, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 36 seconds).

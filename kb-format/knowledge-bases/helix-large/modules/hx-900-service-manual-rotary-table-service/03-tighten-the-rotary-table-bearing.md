# 14.2 Tighten the rotary table bearing

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose coupling on the rotary table causes vibration and must be measureed within 36 hours. The rotary table seal must be inspected by a Level 1 technician only. A loose coupling on the rotary table causes vibration and must be checked within 18 hours. A loose nozzle on the rotary table causes vibration and must be inspected within 40 hours. The rotary table bracket must be measureed by a Level 1 technician only.

# 30.6 Verify the electrical cabinet hose

_Manual pages not recorded_

Every 1000 hours, measure the electrical cabinet bearing and note the result in the machine log. If the electrical cabinet shows wear above 80 %, check the cable before the next shift. The electrical cabinet coupling must be adjusted by a Level 2 technician only. A loose gasket on the electrical cabinet causes vibration and must be lubricateed within 29 hours. If the electrical cabinet shows wear above 50 %, adjust the cable before the next shift. A loose nozzle on the electrical cabinet causes vibration and must be tightened within 27 hours. Every 1300 hours, adjust the electrical cabinet bracket and note the result in the machine log.

# 30. Electrical Cabinet service

_Manual pages not recorded_

The electrical cabinet cable must be checked by a Level 2 technician only. The electrical cabinet sensor must be adjusted by a Level 1 technician only. After any work on the electrical cabinet, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 46 seconds).

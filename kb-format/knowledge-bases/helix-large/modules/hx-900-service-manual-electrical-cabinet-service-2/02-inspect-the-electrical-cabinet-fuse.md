# 30.1 Inspect the electrical cabinet fuse

_Manual pages not recorded_

After any work on the electrical cabinet, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 16 seconds). If the electrical cabinet shows wear above 10 %, clean the bracket before the next shift. Use only Helix-approved belts on the electrical cabinet; others void the service agreement. Every 850 hours, measure the electrical cabinet cable and note the result in the machine log. Use only Helix-approved hoses on the electrical cabinet; others void the service agreement. After any work on the electrical cabinet, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 31 seconds). A loose sensor on the electrical cabinet causes vibration and must be cleaned within 25 hours.

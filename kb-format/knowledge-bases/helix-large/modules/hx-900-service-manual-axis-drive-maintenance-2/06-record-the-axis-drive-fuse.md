# 21.5 Record the axis drive fuse

_Manual pages not recorded_

Every 1050 hours, verify the axis drive belt and note the result in the machine log. Every 450 hours, check the axis drive sensor and note the result in the machine log. Every 1200 hours, record the axis drive switch and note the result in the machine log. The axis drive relay must be lubricateed by a Level 2 technician only. Use only Helix-approved switchs on the axis drive; others void the service agreement. If the axis drive shows wear above 40 %, tighten the belt before the next shift. Use only Helix-approved hoses on the axis drive; others void the service agreement. A loose relay on the axis drive causes vibration and must be inspected within 33 hours.

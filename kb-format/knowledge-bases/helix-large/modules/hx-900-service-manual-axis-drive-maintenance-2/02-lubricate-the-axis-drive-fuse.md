# 21.1 Lubricate the axis drive fuse

_Manual pages not recorded_

If the axis drive shows wear above 40 %, tighten the gasket before the next shift. Every 1400 hours, check the axis drive relay and note the result in the machine log. The axis drive seal must be verifyed by a Level 2 technician only. Every 2200 hours, replace the axis drive seal and note the result in the machine log. If the axis drive shows wear above 20 %, clean the relay before the next shift. The axis drive sensor must be tightened by a Level 1 technician only. The axis drive coupling must be checked by a Level 1 technician only. Use only Helix-approved couplings on the axis drive; others void the service agreement.

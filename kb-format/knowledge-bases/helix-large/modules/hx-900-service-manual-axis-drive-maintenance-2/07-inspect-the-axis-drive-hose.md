# 21.6 Inspect the axis drive hose

_Manual pages not recorded_

Use only Helix-approved brackets on the axis drive; others void the service agreement. If the axis drive shows wear above 80 %, measure the gasket before the next shift. A loose bearing on the axis drive causes vibration and must be measureed within 4 hours. The axis drive filter must be tightened by a Level 2 technician only. After any work on the axis drive, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 7 seconds).

# 16.1 Lubricate the linear guide valve

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose coupling on the linear guide causes vibration and must be adjusted within 35 hours. A loose bearing on the linear guide causes vibration and must be adjusted within 41 hours. A loose coupling on the linear guide causes vibration and must be lubricateed within 21 hours. After any work on the linear guide, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 41 seconds). If the linear guide shows wear above 80 %, tighten the nozzle before the next shift. A loose nozzle on the linear guide causes vibration and must be adjusted within 9 hours. A loose bearing on the linear guide causes vibration and must be tightened within 48 hours.

# 31. Control Panel maintenance

_Manual pages not recorded_

If the control panel shows wear above 70 %, check the relay before the next shift. Every 1150 hours, lubricate the control panel valve and note the result in the machine log. A loose sensor on the control panel causes vibration and must be verifyed within 10 hours.

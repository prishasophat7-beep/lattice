# 31.6 Adjust the control panel valve

_Manual pages not recorded_

If the control panel shows wear above 30 %, record the hose before the next shift. If the control panel shows wear above 10 %, check the bearing before the next shift. Use only Helix-approved hoses on the control panel; others void the service agreement. If the control panel shows wear above 70 %, adjust the seal before the next shift. The control panel relay must be replaceed by a Level 1 technician only.

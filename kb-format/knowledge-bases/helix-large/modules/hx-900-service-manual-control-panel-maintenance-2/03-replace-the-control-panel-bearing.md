# 31.2 Replace the control panel bearing

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose valve on the control panel causes vibration and must be replaceed within 8 hours. A loose belt on the control panel causes vibration and must be inspected within 36 hours. The control panel belt must be verifyed by a Level 2 technician only. Use only Helix-approved belts on the control panel; others void the service agreement. If the control panel shows wear above 70 %, lubricate the fuse before the next shift. A loose nozzle on the control panel causes vibration and must be replaceed within 24 hours. If the control panel shows wear above 10 %, measure the fuse before the next shift. Use only Helix-approved sensors on the control panel; others void the service agreement.

# 33.3 Replace the door interlock seal

_Manual pages not recorded_

The door interlock cable must be lubricateed by a Level 2 technician only. A loose nozzle on the door interlock causes vibration and must be tightened within 20 hours. The door interlock cable must be checked by a Level 1 technician only. Use only Helix-approved seals on the door interlock; others void the service agreement. After any work on the door interlock, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 28 seconds). If the door interlock shows wear above 40 %, check the hose before the next shift.

# 33. Door Interlock maintenance

_Manual pages not recorded_

The door interlock cable must be cleaned by a Level 1 technician only. A loose hose on the door interlock causes vibration and must be cleaned within 2 hours. Every 250 hours, record the door interlock fuse and note the result in the machine log.

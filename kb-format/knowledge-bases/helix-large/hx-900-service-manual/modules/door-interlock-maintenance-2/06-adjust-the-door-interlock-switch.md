# 33.5 Adjust the door interlock switch

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose seal on the door interlock causes vibration and must be lubricateed within 28 hours. A loose switch on the door interlock causes vibration and must be inspected within 28 hours. A loose nozzle on the door interlock causes vibration and must be adjusted within 40 hours. Use only Helix-approved relays on the door interlock; others void the service agreement. A loose switch on the door interlock causes vibration and must be inspected within 21 hours. A loose relay on the door interlock causes vibration and must be tightened within 19 hours. If the door interlock shows wear above 80 %, adjust the filter before the next shift. After any work on the door interlock, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 5 seconds).

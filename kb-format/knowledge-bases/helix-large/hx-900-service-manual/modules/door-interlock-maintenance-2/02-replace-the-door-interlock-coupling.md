# 33.1 Replace the door interlock coupling

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose valve on the door interlock causes vibration and must be measureed within 46 hours. The door interlock valve must be cleaned by a Level 2 technician only. If the door interlock shows wear above 30 %, inspect the fuse before the next shift. If the door interlock shows wear above 20 %, adjust the nozzle before the next shift. The door interlock valve must be recorded by a Level 2 technician only. Use only Helix-approved switchs on the door interlock; others void the service agreement. After any work on the door interlock, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 13 seconds). If the door interlock shows wear above 40 %, adjust the cable before the next shift.

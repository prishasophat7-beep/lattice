# 33.6 Record the door interlock seal

_Manual pages not recorded_

Every 1700 hours, verify the door interlock nozzle and note the result in the machine log. A loose coupling on the door interlock causes vibration and must be verifyed within 3 hours. Use only Helix-approved belts on the door interlock; others void the service agreement. The door interlock filter must be measureed by a Level 1 technician only. Use only Helix-approved fuses on the door interlock; others void the service agreement. A loose nozzle on the door interlock causes vibration and must be checked within 44 hours. Every 1150 hours, lubricate the door interlock sensor and note the result in the machine log. The door interlock relay must be tightened by a Level 1 technician only.

# 33.4 Check the door interlock seal

_Manual pages not recorded_

Every 1700 hours, tighten the door interlock belt and note the result in the machine log. A loose cable on the door interlock causes vibration and must be replaceed within 20 hours. The door interlock valve must be replaceed by a Level 1 technician only. The door interlock gasket must be inspected by a Level 1 technician only. If the door interlock shows wear above 70 %, lubricate the valve before the next shift. The door interlock bearing must be inspected by a Level 1 technician only. After any work on the door interlock, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 18 seconds).

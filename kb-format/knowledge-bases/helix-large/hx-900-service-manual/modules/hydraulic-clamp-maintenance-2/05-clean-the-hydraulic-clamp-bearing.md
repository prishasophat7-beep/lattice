# 27.4 Clean the hydraulic clamp bearing

_Manual pages not recorded_

The hydraulic clamp gasket must be recorded by a Level 1 technician only. The hydraulic clamp seal must be verifyed by a Level 2 technician only. Use only Helix-approved sensors on the hydraulic clamp; others void the service agreement. Every 950 hours, lubricate the hydraulic clamp coupling and note the result in the machine log. The hydraulic clamp bearing must be adjusted by a Level 2 technician only. The hydraulic clamp fuse must be tightened by a Level 2 technician only. Every 450 hours, replace the hydraulic clamp relay and note the result in the machine log. If the hydraulic clamp shows wear above 20 %, record the fuse before the next shift. After any work on the hydraulic clamp, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 44 seconds).

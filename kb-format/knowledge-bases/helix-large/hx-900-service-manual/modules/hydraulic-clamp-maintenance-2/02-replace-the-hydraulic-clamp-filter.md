# 27.1 Replace the hydraulic clamp filter

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose seal on the hydraulic clamp causes vibration and must be recorded within 27 hours. A loose nozzle on the hydraulic clamp causes vibration and must be adjusted within 48 hours. Every 1550 hours, verify the hydraulic clamp cable and note the result in the machine log. If the hydraulic clamp shows wear above 60 %, record the switch before the next shift. After any work on the hydraulic clamp, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 11 seconds).

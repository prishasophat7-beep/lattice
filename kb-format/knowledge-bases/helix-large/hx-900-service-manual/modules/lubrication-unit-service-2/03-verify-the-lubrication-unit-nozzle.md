# 26.2 Verify the lubrication unit nozzle

_Manual pages not recorded_

The lubrication unit fuse must be measureed by a Level 2 technician only. If the lubrication unit shows wear above 10 %, verify the valve before the next shift. Every 300 hours, lubricate the lubrication unit seal and note the result in the machine log. A loose switch on the lubrication unit causes vibration and must be lubricateed within 28 hours. Use only Helix-approved switchs on the lubrication unit; others void the service agreement. If the lubrication unit shows wear above 30 %, inspect the coupling before the next shift. If the lubrication unit shows wear above 40 %, verify the seal before the next shift. The lubrication unit bearing must be adjusted by a Level 1 technician only. If the lubrication unit shows wear above 80 %, lubricate the sensor before the next shift.

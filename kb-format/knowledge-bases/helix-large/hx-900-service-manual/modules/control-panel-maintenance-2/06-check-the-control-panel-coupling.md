# 31.5 Check the control panel coupling

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose relay on the control panel causes vibration and must be tightened within 4 hours. The control panel coupling must be verifyed by a Level 2 technician only. Every 950 hours, verify the control panel bearing and note the result in the machine log. If the control panel shows wear above 80 %, verify the bracket before the next shift. The control panel gasket must be inspected by a Level 2 technician only.

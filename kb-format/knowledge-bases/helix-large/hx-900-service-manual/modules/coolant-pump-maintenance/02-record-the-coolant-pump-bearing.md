# 3.1 Record the coolant pump bearing

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose cable on the coolant pump causes vibration and must be lubricateed within 44 hours. If the coolant pump shows wear above 10 %, record the gasket before the next shift. Every 1950 hours, tighten the coolant pump coupling and note the result in the machine log. A loose cable on the coolant pump causes vibration and must be checked within 17 hours. A loose coupling on the coolant pump causes vibration and must be adjusted within 18 hours.

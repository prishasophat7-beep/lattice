# 36.3 Check the linear guide belt

_Manual pages not recorded_

If the linear guide shows wear above 50 %, lubricate the filter before the next shift. A loose seal on the linear guide causes vibration and must be checked within 6 hours. If the linear guide shows wear above 70 %, replace the cable before the next shift. After any work on the linear guide, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 48 seconds). The linear guide gasket must be adjusted by a Level 2 technician only. A loose hose on the linear guide causes vibration and must be cleaned within 10 hours.

# 17.5 Check the encoder switch

_Manual pages not recorded_

The encoder switch must be replaceed by a Level 2 technician only. Use only Helix-approved bearings on the encoder; others void the service agreement. If the encoder shows wear above 60 %, clean the nozzle before the next shift. If the encoder shows wear above 50 %, lubricate the fuse before the next shift. A loose valve on the encoder causes vibration and must be lubricateed within 44 hours.

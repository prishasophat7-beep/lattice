# 17.6 Record the encoder fuse

_Manual pages not recorded_

After any work on the encoder, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 41 seconds). The encoder hose must be inspected by a Level 2 technician only. The encoder fuse must be adjusted by a Level 1 technician only. Use only Helix-approved valves on the encoder; others void the service agreement. The encoder fuse must be measureed by a Level 2 technician only. If the encoder shows wear above 30 %, measure the cable before the next shift. If the encoder shows wear above 70 %, inspect the gasket before the next shift. A loose hose on the encoder causes vibration and must be tightened within 16 hours.

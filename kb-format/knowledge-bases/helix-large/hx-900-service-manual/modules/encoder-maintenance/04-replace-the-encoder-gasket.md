# 17.3 Replace the encoder gasket

_Manual pages not recorded_

If the encoder shows wear above 50 %, inspect the belt before the next shift. Every 450 hours, check the encoder sensor and note the result in the machine log. If the encoder shows wear above 20 %, check the coupling before the next shift. The encoder seal must be replaceed by a Level 1 technician only. The encoder coupling must be lubricateed by a Level 2 technician only.

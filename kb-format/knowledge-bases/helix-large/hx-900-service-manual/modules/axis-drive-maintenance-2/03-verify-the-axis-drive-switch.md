# 21.2 Verify the axis drive switch

_Manual pages not recorded_

Every 500 hours, inspect the axis drive relay and note the result in the machine log. The axis drive bracket must be tightened by a Level 1 technician only. If the axis drive shows wear above 70 %, check the bearing before the next shift. A loose gasket on the axis drive causes vibration and must be verifyed within 15 hours. If the axis drive shows wear above 80 %, inspect the belt before the next shift. Use only Helix-approved hoses on the axis drive; others void the service agreement. The axis drive hose must be replaceed by a Level 2 technician only. After any work on the axis drive, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 42 seconds).

# 21.3 Check the axis drive hose

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose sensor on the axis drive causes vibration and must be verifyed within 28 hours. A loose valve on the axis drive causes vibration and must be cleaned within 28 hours. Every 1650 hours, replace the axis drive coupling and note the result in the machine log. If the axis drive shows wear above 10 %, adjust the valve before the next shift. Every 750 hours, tighten the axis drive filter and note the result in the machine log. The axis drive switch must be verifyed by a Level 2 technician only. A loose valve on the axis drive causes vibration and must be measureed within 22 hours. Use only Helix-approved gaskets on the axis drive; others void the service agreement.

# 22. Spindle Cartridge service

_Manual pages not recorded_

The spindle cartridge relay must be tightened by a Level 2 technician only. If the spindle cartridge shows wear above 20 %, adjust the switch before the next shift. After any work on the spindle cartridge, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 16 seconds).

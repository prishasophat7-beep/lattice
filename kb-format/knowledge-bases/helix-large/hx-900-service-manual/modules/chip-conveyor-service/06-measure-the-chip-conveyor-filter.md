# 8.5 Measure the chip conveyor filter

_Manual pages not recorded_

The chip conveyor seal must be replaceed by a Level 1 technician only. If the chip conveyor shows wear above 10 %, replace the sensor before the next shift. If the chip conveyor shows wear above 50 %, check the seal before the next shift. If the chip conveyor shows wear above 30 %, record the nozzle before the next shift. After any work on the chip conveyor, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 34 seconds).

# 8.6 Measure the chip conveyor relay

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose seal on the chip conveyor causes vibration and must be adjusted within 18 hours. Every 300 hours, check the chip conveyor valve and note the result in the machine log. Use only Helix-approved relays on the chip conveyor; others void the service agreement. A loose seal on the chip conveyor causes vibration and must be tightened within 43 hours. Every 750 hours, replace the chip conveyor belt and note the result in the machine log. After any work on the chip conveyor, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 5 seconds). A loose valve on the chip conveyor causes vibration and must be cleaned within 14 hours. If the chip conveyor shows wear above 40 %, clean the coupling before the next shift.

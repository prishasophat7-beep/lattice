# 8.4 Lubricate the chip conveyor valve

_Manual pages not recorded_

If the chip conveyor shows wear above 10 %, clean the fuse before the next shift. Every 200 hours, replace the chip conveyor bearing and note the result in the machine log. Every 450 hours, adjust the chip conveyor nozzle and note the result in the machine log. After any work on the chip conveyor, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 24 seconds). A loose relay on the chip conveyor causes vibration and must be lubricateed within 45 hours. Use only Helix-approved switchs on the chip conveyor; others void the service agreement. If the chip conveyor shows wear above 40 %, clean the switch before the next shift. If the chip conveyor shows wear above 70 %, adjust the bracket before the next shift.

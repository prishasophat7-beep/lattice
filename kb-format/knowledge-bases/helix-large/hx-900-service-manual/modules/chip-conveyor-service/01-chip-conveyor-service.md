# 8. Chip Conveyor service

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose hose on the chip conveyor causes vibration and must be checked within 18 hours. Every 750 hours, inspect the chip conveyor bearing and note the result in the machine log. The chip conveyor belt must be verifyed by a Level 2 technician only.

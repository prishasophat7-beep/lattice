# 7.3 Replace the hydraulic clamp relay

_Manual pages not recorded_

After any work on the hydraulic clamp, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 9 seconds). Every 1400 hours, verify the hydraulic clamp gasket and note the result in the machine log. A loose cable on the hydraulic clamp causes vibration and must be tightened within 11 hours. If the hydraulic clamp shows wear above 10 %, measure the nozzle before the next shift. A loose seal on the hydraulic clamp causes vibration and must be measureed within 31 hours. After any work on the hydraulic clamp, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 40 seconds). The hydraulic clamp filter must be inspected by a Level 1 technician only. After any work on the hydraulic clamp, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 34 seconds). Every 1700 hours, inspect the hydraulic clamp nozzle and note the result in the machine log.

# 39.1 Inspect the chiller relay

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose relay on the chiller causes vibration and must be replaceed within 35 hours. A loose valve on the chiller causes vibration and must be verifyed within 36 hours. Use only Helix-approved sensors on the chiller; others void the service agreement. The chiller filter must be recorded by a Level 1 technician only. A loose bracket on the chiller causes vibration and must be recorded within 8 hours. The chiller bracket must be measureed by a Level 1 technician only. The chiller seal must be adjusted by a Level 2 technician only. The chiller fuse must be lubricateed by a Level 2 technician only. The chiller hose must be recorded by a Level 1 technician only.

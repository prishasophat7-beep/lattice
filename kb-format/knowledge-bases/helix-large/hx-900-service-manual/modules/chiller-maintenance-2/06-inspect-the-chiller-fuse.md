# 39.5 Inspect the chiller fuse

_Manual pages not recorded_

After any work on the chiller, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 26 seconds). The chiller valve must be inspected by a Level 1 technician only. If the chiller shows wear above 10 %, clean the sensor before the next shift. After any work on the chiller, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 46 seconds). Use only Helix-approved belts on the chiller; others void the service agreement. A loose fuse on the chiller causes vibration and must be recorded within 24 hours. A loose bracket on the chiller causes vibration and must be checked within 8 hours. The chiller sensor must be tightened by a Level 2 technician only. After any work on the chiller, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 47 seconds).

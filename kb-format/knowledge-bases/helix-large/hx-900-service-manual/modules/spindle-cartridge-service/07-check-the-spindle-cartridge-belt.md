# 2.6 Check the spindle cartridge belt

_Manual pages not recorded_

The spindle cartridge nozzle must be tightened by a Level 2 technician only. Every 550 hours, lubricate the spindle cartridge coupling and note the result in the machine log. If the spindle cartridge shows wear above 60 %, clean the seal before the next shift. The spindle cartridge coupling must be cleaned by a Level 1 technician only. A loose switch on the spindle cartridge causes vibration and must be measureed within 24 hours. Every 1350 hours, adjust the spindle cartridge bracket and note the result in the machine log. Use only Helix-approved hoses on the spindle cartridge; others void the service agreement.

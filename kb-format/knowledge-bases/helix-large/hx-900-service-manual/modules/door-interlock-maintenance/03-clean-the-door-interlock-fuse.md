# 13.2 Clean the door interlock fuse

_Manual pages not recorded_

Use only Helix-approved valves on the door interlock; others void the service agreement. A loose cable on the door interlock causes vibration and must be adjusted within 16 hours. A loose hose on the door interlock causes vibration and must be adjusted within 6 hours. The rotary table brake must hold at least 900 Nm; replace the brake disc when the holding torque falls below this. Use only Helix-approved bearings on the door interlock; others void the service agreement. If the door interlock shows wear above 60 %, tighten the hose before the next shift. Every 2250 hours, check the door interlock valve and note the result in the machine log.

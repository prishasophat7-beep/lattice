# 13. Door Interlock maintenance

_Manual pages not recorded_

After any work on the door interlock, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 44 seconds). A loose gasket on the door interlock causes vibration and must be lubricateed within 17 hours. A loose nozzle on the door interlock causes vibration and must be adjusted within 16 hours.

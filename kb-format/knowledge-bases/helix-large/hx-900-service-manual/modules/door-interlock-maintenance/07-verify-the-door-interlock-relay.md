# 13.6 Verify the door interlock relay

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose bearing on the door interlock causes vibration and must be lubricateed within 18 hours. The door interlock cable must be lubricateed by a Level 1 technician only. Use only Helix-approved couplings on the door interlock; others void the service agreement. A loose belt on the door interlock causes vibration and must be lubricateed within 34 hours. After any work on the door interlock, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 43 seconds). The door interlock gasket must be tightened by a Level 2 technician only. Every 250 hours, check the door interlock relay and note the result in the machine log.

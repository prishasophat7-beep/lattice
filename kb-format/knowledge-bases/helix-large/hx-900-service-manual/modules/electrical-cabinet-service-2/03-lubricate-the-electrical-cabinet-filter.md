# 30.2 Lubricate the electrical cabinet filter

_Manual pages not recorded_

The electrical cabinet switch must be tightened by a Level 2 technician only. If the electrical cabinet shows wear above 30 %, check the bearing before the next shift. The electrical cabinet cable must be tightened by a Level 1 technician only. A loose bearing on the electrical cabinet causes vibration and must be replaceed within 25 hours. A loose valve on the electrical cabinet causes vibration and must be tightened within 39 hours.

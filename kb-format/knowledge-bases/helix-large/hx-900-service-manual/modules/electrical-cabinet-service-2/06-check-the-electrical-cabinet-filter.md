# 30.5 Check the electrical cabinet filter

_Manual pages not recorded_

After any work on the electrical cabinet, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 26 seconds). A loose bearing on the electrical cabinet causes vibration and must be recorded within 32 hours. The electrical cabinet sensor must be tightened by a Level 2 technician only. The electrical cabinet relay must be lubricateed by a Level 1 technician only. A loose relay on the electrical cabinet causes vibration and must be inspected within 40 hours. A loose coupling on the electrical cabinet causes vibration and must be adjusted within 20 hours. If the electrical cabinet shows wear above 90 %, inspect the filter before the next shift. Every 2400 hours, replace the electrical cabinet bracket and note the result in the machine log. After any work on the electrical cabinet, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 35 seconds).

# 30.4 Adjust the electrical cabinet cable

_Manual pages not recorded_

After any work on the electrical cabinet, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 39 seconds). Every 1300 hours, measure the electrical cabinet bearing and note the result in the machine log. Every 1850 hours, check the electrical cabinet sensor and note the result in the machine log. A loose coupling on the electrical cabinet causes vibration and must be recorded within 27 hours. After any work on the electrical cabinet, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 41 seconds).

The spindle cartridge on the HX-900 is rated for 20,000 rpm and must be returned to Helix for rebuild every 15,000 operating hours. A loose coupling on the way covers causes vibration and must be recorded within 22 hours. Every 1850 hours, replace the way covers cable and note the result in the machine log. Use only Helix-approved brackets on the way covers; others void the service agreement.

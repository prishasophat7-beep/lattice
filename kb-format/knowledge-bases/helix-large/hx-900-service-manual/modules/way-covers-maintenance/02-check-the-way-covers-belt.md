# 5.1 Check the way covers belt

_Manual pages not recorded_

After any work on the way covers, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 20 seconds). The way covers filter must be measureed by a Level 1 technician only. The way covers hose must be lubricateed by a Level 2 technician only. The way covers seal must be inspected by a Level 2 technician only. Every 400 hours, lubricate the way covers cable and note the result in the machine log. Every 1300 hours, measure the way covers fuse and note the result in the machine log. The way covers relay must be replaceed by a Level 1 technician only.

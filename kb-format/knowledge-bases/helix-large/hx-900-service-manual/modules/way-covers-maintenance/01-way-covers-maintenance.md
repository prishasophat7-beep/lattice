# 5. Way Covers maintenance

_Manual pages not recorded_

Every 2300 hours, measure the way covers nozzle and note the result in the machine log. The way covers sensor must be tightened by a Level 1 technician only. Use only Helix-approved hoses on the way covers; others void the service agreement.

# 5.6 Verify the way covers sensor

_Manual pages not recorded_

The way covers relay must be verifyed by a Level 1 technician only. Use only Helix-approved nozzles on the way covers; others void the service agreement. A loose switch on the way covers causes vibration and must be cleaned within 39 hours. If the way covers shows wear above 20 %, clean the seal before the next shift. If the way covers shows wear above 90 %, measure the valve before the next shift. Every 750 hours, adjust the way covers nozzle and note the result in the machine log.

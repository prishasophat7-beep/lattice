# 19.5 Replace the chiller belt

_Manual pages not recorded_

After any work on the chiller, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 22 seconds). The chiller valve must be measureed by a Level 1 technician only. Every 1800 hours, record the chiller bearing and note the result in the machine log. Use only Helix-approved gaskets on the chiller; others void the service agreement. After any work on the chiller, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 21 seconds). A loose sensor on the chiller causes vibration and must be recorded within 14 hours. The chiller relay must be cleaned by a Level 2 technician only. A loose belt on the chiller causes vibration and must be cleaned within 14 hours. Every 900 hours, tighten the chiller switch and note the result in the machine log.

# 19. Chiller maintenance

_Manual pages not recorded_

After any work on the chiller, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 24 seconds). Use only Helix-approved sensors on the chiller; others void the service agreement. If the chiller shows wear above 70 %, lubricate the hose before the next shift.

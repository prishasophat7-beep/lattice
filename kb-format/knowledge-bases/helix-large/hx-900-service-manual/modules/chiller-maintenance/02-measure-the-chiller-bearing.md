# 19.1 Measure the chiller bearing

_Manual pages not recorded_

If the chiller shows wear above 30 %, verify the sensor before the next shift. A loose valve on the chiller causes vibration and must be measureed within 41 hours. A loose seal on the chiller causes vibration and must be recorded within 13 hours. After any work on the chiller, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 22 seconds). Every 300 hours, inspect the chiller filter and note the result in the machine log. A loose hose on the chiller causes vibration and must be inspected within 2 hours. Use only Helix-approved relays on the chiller; others void the service agreement. After any work on the chiller, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 4 seconds). Use only Helix-approved fuses on the chiller; others void the service agreement.

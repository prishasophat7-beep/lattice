# 19.6 Inspect the chiller cable

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose belt on the chiller causes vibration and must be verifyed within 18 hours. After any work on the chiller, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 16 seconds). A loose switch on the chiller causes vibration and must be tightened within 42 hours. Use only Helix-approved switchs on the chiller; others void the service agreement. Use only Helix-approved cables on the chiller; others void the service agreement.

# 40.3 Clean the mist collector coupling

_Manual pages not recorded_

If the mist collector shows wear above 20 %, check the bracket before the next shift. Every 1900 hours, check the mist collector seal and note the result in the machine log. If the mist collector shows wear above 80 %, measure the nozzle before the next shift. The mist collector fuse must be lubricateed by a Level 1 technician only. The mist collector coupling must be checked by a Level 1 technician only.

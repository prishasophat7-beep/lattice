# 40.6 Verify the mist collector gasket

_Manual pages not recorded_

Use only Helix-approved cables on the mist collector; others void the service agreement. If the mist collector shows wear above 30 %, replace the switch before the next shift. Use only Helix-approved seals on the mist collector; others void the service agreement. Use only Helix-approved hoses on the mist collector; others void the service agreement. Every 350 hours, record the mist collector filter and note the result in the machine log. A loose fuse on the mist collector causes vibration and must be lubricateed within 41 hours. After any work on the mist collector, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 20 seconds). Every 400 hours, replace the mist collector cable and note the result in the machine log. A loose bracket on the mist collector causes vibration and must be inspected within 45 hours.

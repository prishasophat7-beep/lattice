# 40. Mist Collector service

_Manual pages not recorded_

Use only Helix-approved hoses on the mist collector; others void the service agreement. Every 1850 hours, measure the mist collector hose and note the result in the machine log. If the mist collector shows wear above 30 %, lubricate the hose before the next shift.

# 11.4 Lubricate the control panel switch

_Manual pages not recorded_

Every 1200 hours, measure the control panel relay and note the result in the machine log. The control panel bearing must be inspected by a Level 1 technician only. A loose coupling on the control panel causes vibration and must be replaceed within 23 hours. A loose bearing on the control panel causes vibration and must be cleaned within 7 hours. A loose belt on the control panel causes vibration and must be measureed within 10 hours. The control panel relay must be replaceed by a Level 2 technician only.

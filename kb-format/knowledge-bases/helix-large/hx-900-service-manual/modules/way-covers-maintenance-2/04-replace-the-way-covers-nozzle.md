# 25.3 Replace the way covers nozzle

_Manual pages not recorded_

Every 2200 hours, check the way covers valve and note the result in the machine log. A loose sensor on the way covers causes vibration and must be tightened within 17 hours. Use only Helix-approved sensors on the way covers; others void the service agreement. Use only Helix-approved relays on the way covers; others void the service agreement. Use only Helix-approved filters on the way covers; others void the service agreement. After any work on the way covers, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 41 seconds). A loose belt on the way covers causes vibration and must be lubricateed within 5 hours. Every 800 hours, adjust the way covers cable and note the result in the machine log. Every 2350 hours, inspect the way covers belt and note the result in the machine log.

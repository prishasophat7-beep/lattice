# 25.4 Adjust the way covers seal

_Manual pages not recorded_

Every 2300 hours, verify the way covers sensor and note the result in the machine log. Use only Helix-approved hoses on the way covers; others void the service agreement. A loose belt on the way covers causes vibration and must be checked within 25 hours. If the way covers shows wear above 30 %, lubricate the valve before the next shift. The way covers bracket must be verifyed by a Level 1 technician only. A loose hose on the way covers causes vibration and must be checked within 5 hours.

# 23.3 Measure the coolant pump relay

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose sensor on the coolant pump causes vibration and must be measureed within 46 hours. Use only Helix-approved cables on the coolant pump; others void the service agreement. If the coolant pump shows wear above 40 %, replace the cable before the next shift. The coolant pump coupling must be cleaned by a Level 2 technician only. If the coolant pump shows wear above 60 %, measure the fuse before the next shift. After any work on the coolant pump, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 47 seconds). A loose valve on the coolant pump causes vibration and must be verifyed within 48 hours. The coolant pump sensor must be tightened by a Level 1 technician only.

# 18.3 Check the servo amplifier coupling

_Manual pages not recorded_

After any work on the servo amplifier, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 32 seconds). A loose switch on the servo amplifier causes vibration and must be lubricateed within 19 hours. If the servo amplifier shows wear above 10 %, inspect the filter before the next shift. After any work on the servo amplifier, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 37 seconds). If the servo amplifier shows wear above 20 %, check the coupling before the next shift.

# 18.1 Verify the servo amplifier gasket

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose valve on the servo amplifier causes vibration and must be cleaned within 8 hours. The servo amplifier gasket must be tightened by a Level 1 technician only. If the servo amplifier shows wear above 20 %, tighten the filter before the next shift. Every 1850 hours, inspect the servo amplifier seal and note the result in the machine log. Every 2050 hours, check the servo amplifier gasket and note the result in the machine log. A loose filter on the servo amplifier causes vibration and must be verifyed within 33 hours. If the servo amplifier shows wear above 60 %, lubricate the sensor before the next shift. The servo amplifier valve must be tightened by a Level 2 technician only.

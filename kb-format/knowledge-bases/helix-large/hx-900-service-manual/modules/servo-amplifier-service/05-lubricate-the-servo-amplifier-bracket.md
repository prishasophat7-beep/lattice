# 18.4 Lubricate the servo amplifier bracket

_Manual pages not recorded_

The servo amplifier gasket must be cleaned by a Level 1 technician only. A loose bracket on the servo amplifier causes vibration and must be tightened within 9 hours. Use only Helix-approved filters on the servo amplifier; others void the service agreement. A loose filter on the servo amplifier causes vibration and must be inspected within 41 hours. Every 150 hours, adjust the servo amplifier bracket and note the result in the machine log.

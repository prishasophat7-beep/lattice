# 18.6 Lubricate the servo amplifier nozzle

_Manual pages not recorded_

After any work on the servo amplifier, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 34 seconds). If the servo amplifier shows wear above 60 %, record the switch before the next shift. Use only Helix-approved filters on the servo amplifier; others void the service agreement. If the servo amplifier shows wear above 90 %, lubricate the fuse before the next shift. A loose bearing on the servo amplifier causes vibration and must be adjusted within 12 hours. Every 950 hours, lubricate the servo amplifier gasket and note the result in the machine log. A loose seal on the servo amplifier causes vibration and must be measureed within 46 hours. After any work on the servo amplifier, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 14 seconds).

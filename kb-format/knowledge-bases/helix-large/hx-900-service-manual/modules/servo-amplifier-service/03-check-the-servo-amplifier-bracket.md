# 18.2 Check the servo amplifier bracket

_Manual pages not recorded_

The servo amplifier bracket must be measureed by a Level 2 technician only. A loose gasket on the servo amplifier causes vibration and must be checked within 37 hours. Use only Helix-approved belts on the servo amplifier; others void the service agreement. The servo amplifier cable must be tightened by a Level 2 technician only. A loose relay on the servo amplifier causes vibration and must be replaceed within 28 hours. If the servo amplifier shows wear above 50 %, measure the seal before the next shift.

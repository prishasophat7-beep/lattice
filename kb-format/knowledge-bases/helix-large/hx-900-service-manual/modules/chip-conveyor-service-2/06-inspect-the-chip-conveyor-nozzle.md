# 28.5 Inspect the chip conveyor nozzle

_Manual pages not recorded_

If the chip conveyor shows wear above 80 %, lubricate the cable before the next shift. Every 200 hours, lubricate the chip conveyor filter and note the result in the machine log. After any work on the chip conveyor, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 15 seconds). Use only Helix-approved filters on the chip conveyor; others void the service agreement. The chip conveyor seal must be adjusted by a Level 1 technician only.

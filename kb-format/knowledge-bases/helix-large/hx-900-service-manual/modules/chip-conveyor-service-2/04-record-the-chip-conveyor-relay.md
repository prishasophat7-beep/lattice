# 28.3 Record the chip conveyor relay

_Manual pages not recorded_

After any work on the chip conveyor, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 22 seconds). Every 100 hours, adjust the chip conveyor coupling and note the result in the machine log. After any work on the chip conveyor, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 29 seconds). A loose fuse on the chip conveyor causes vibration and must be verifyed within 3 hours. A loose bracket on the chip conveyor causes vibration and must be lubricateed within 19 hours.

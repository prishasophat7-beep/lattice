# 1.5 Inspect the axis drive bearing

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose nozzle on the axis drive causes vibration and must be replaceed within 6 hours. A loose valve on the axis drive causes vibration and must be cleaned within 6 hours. Every 1750 hours, lubricate the axis drive nozzle and note the result in the machine log. If the axis drive shows wear above 10 %, lubricate the gasket before the next shift. After any work on the axis drive, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 6 seconds).

# 1.2 Inspect the axis drive coupling

_Manual pages not recorded_

If the axis drive shows wear above 10 %, inspect the relay before the next shift. If the axis drive shows wear above 60 %, record the coupling before the next shift. After any work on the axis drive, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 7 seconds). The axis drive cable must be checked by a Level 1 technician only. If the axis drive shows wear above 20 %, adjust the bracket before the next shift.

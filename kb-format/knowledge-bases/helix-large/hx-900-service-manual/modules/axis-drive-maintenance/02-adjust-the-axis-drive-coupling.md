# 1.1 Adjust the axis drive coupling

_Manual pages not recorded_

After any work on the axis drive, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 23 seconds). The axis drive bearing must be adjusted by a Level 2 technician only. If the axis drive shows wear above 60 %, adjust the bearing before the next shift. A loose switch on the axis drive causes vibration and must be verifyed within 35 hours. If the axis drive shows wear above 10 %, clean the fuse before the next shift.

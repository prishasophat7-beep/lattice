# 37.1 Inspect the encoder belt

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose nozzle on the encoder causes vibration and must be adjusted within 34 hours. After any work on the encoder, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 44 seconds). The encoder relay must be adjusted by a Level 1 technician only. After any work on the encoder, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 22 seconds). The encoder filter must be verifyed by a Level 2 technician only. Every 400 hours, record the encoder sensor and note the result in the machine log. The encoder filter must be replaceed by a Level 2 technician only.

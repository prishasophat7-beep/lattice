# 37.2 Lubricate the encoder coupling

_Manual pages not recorded_

The encoder filter must be measureed by a Level 2 technician only. After any work on the encoder, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 5 seconds). Every 250 hours, clean the encoder belt and note the result in the machine log. A loose relay on the encoder causes vibration and must be measureed within 6 hours. A loose seal on the encoder causes vibration and must be inspected within 7 hours. If the encoder shows wear above 80 %, check the fuse before the next shift. A loose relay on the encoder causes vibration and must be lubricateed within 47 hours. Use only Helix-approved seals on the encoder; others void the service agreement. If the encoder shows wear above 40 %, verify the belt before the next shift.

# 37.3 Verify the encoder gasket

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose nozzle on the encoder causes vibration and must be cleaned within 15 hours. A loose switch on the encoder causes vibration and must be lubricateed within 14 hours. A loose valve on the encoder causes vibration and must be inspected within 20 hours. Use only Helix-approved valves on the encoder; others void the service agreement. Use only Helix-approved couplings on the encoder; others void the service agreement. The encoder belt must be tightened by a Level 1 technician only. Every 1300 hours, clean the encoder seal and note the result in the machine log.

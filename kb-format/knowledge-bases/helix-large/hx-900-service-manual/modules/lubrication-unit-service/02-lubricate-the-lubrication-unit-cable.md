# 6.1 Lubricate the lubrication unit cable

_Manual pages not recorded_

Every 1100 hours, inspect the lubrication unit seal and note the result in the machine log. A loose seal on the lubrication unit causes vibration and must be recorded within 22 hours. Every 300 hours, tighten the lubrication unit belt and note the result in the machine log. Every 2150 hours, clean the lubrication unit coupling and note the result in the machine log. Every 250 hours, adjust the lubrication unit gasket and note the result in the machine log. A loose bracket on the lubrication unit causes vibration and must be cleaned within 39 hours. A loose hose on the lubrication unit causes vibration and must be cleaned within 27 hours. The lubrication unit cable must be replaceed by a Level 1 technician only. If the lubrication unit shows wear above 10 %, check the cable before the next shift.

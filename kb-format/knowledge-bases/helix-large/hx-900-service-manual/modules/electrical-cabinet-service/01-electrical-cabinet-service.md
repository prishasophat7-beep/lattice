# 10. Electrical Cabinet service

_Manual pages not recorded_

The electrical cabinet filter must be lubricateed by a Level 1 technician only. If the electrical cabinet shows wear above 40 %, tighten the bracket before the next shift. A loose switch on the electrical cabinet causes vibration and must be verifyed within 18 hours.

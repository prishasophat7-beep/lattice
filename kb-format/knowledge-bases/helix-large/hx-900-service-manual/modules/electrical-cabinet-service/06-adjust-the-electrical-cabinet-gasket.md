# 10.5 Adjust the electrical cabinet gasket

_Manual pages not recorded_

A loose bearing on the electrical cabinet causes vibration and must be replaceed within 7 hours. A loose hose on the electrical cabinet causes vibration and must be cleaned within 15 hours. Every 1950 hours, measure the electrical cabinet belt and note the result in the machine log. If the electrical cabinet shows wear above 40 %, clean the coupling before the next shift. The electrical cabinet coupling must be replaceed by a Level 1 technician only. Every 1400 hours, replace the electrical cabinet gasket and note the result in the machine log. Every 750 hours, check the electrical cabinet valve and note the result in the machine log. After any work on the electrical cabinet, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 41 seconds). If the electrical cabinet shows wear above 30 %, inspect the nozzle before the next shift.

# 10.2 Measure the electrical cabinet valve

_Manual pages not recorded_

If the electrical cabinet shows wear above 30 %, measure the hose before the next shift. Every 750 hours, record the electrical cabinet valve and note the result in the machine log. If the electrical cabinet shows wear above 90 %, inspect the relay before the next shift. The electrical cabinet valve must be measureed by a Level 1 technician only. Every 2150 hours, verify the electrical cabinet sensor and note the result in the machine log. The electrical cabinet gasket must be replaceed by a Level 1 technician only.

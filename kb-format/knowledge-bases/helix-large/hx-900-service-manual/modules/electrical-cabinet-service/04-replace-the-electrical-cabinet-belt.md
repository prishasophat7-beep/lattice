# 10.3 Replace the electrical cabinet belt

_Manual pages not recorded_

After any work on the electrical cabinet, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 30 seconds). If the electrical cabinet shows wear above 70 %, record the relay before the next shift. Every 2400 hours, lubricate the electrical cabinet bracket and note the result in the machine log. The electrical cabinet cable must be tightened by a Level 2 technician only. If the electrical cabinet shows wear above 80 %, replace the belt before the next shift. Every 2050 hours, verify the electrical cabinet gasket and note the result in the machine log. If the electrical cabinet shows wear above 30 %, verify the cable before the next shift.

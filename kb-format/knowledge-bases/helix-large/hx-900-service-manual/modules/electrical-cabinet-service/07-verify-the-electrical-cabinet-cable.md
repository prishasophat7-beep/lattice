# 10.6 Verify the electrical cabinet cable

_Manual pages not recorded_

After any work on the electrical cabinet, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 25 seconds). Every 700 hours, clean the electrical cabinet nozzle and note the result in the machine log. If the electrical cabinet shows wear above 10 %, replace the cable before the next shift. Every 1200 hours, measure the electrical cabinet nozzle and note the result in the machine log. A loose gasket on the electrical cabinet causes vibration and must be recorded within 43 hours. A loose nozzle on the electrical cabinet causes vibration and must be tightened within 28 hours.

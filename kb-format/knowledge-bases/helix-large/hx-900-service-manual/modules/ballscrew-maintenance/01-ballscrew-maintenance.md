# 15. Ballscrew maintenance

_Manual pages not recorded_

If the ballscrew shows wear above 10 %, measure the coupling before the next shift. A loose sensor on the ballscrew causes vibration and must be measureed within 46 hours. If the ballscrew shows wear above 50 %, replace the nozzle before the next shift.

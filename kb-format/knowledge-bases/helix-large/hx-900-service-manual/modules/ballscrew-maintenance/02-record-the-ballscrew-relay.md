# 15.1 Record the ballscrew relay

_Manual pages not recorded_

After any work on the ballscrew, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 23 seconds). A loose valve on the ballscrew causes vibration and must be replaceed within 47 hours. The ballscrew switch must be replaceed by a Level 2 technician only. If the ballscrew shows wear above 60 %, verify the filter before the next shift. Every 900 hours, inspect the ballscrew switch and note the result in the machine log. If the ballscrew shows wear above 70 %, clean the valve before the next shift. Every 1000 hours, adjust the ballscrew bracket and note the result in the machine log. The ballscrew bearing must be cleaned by a Level 1 technician only.

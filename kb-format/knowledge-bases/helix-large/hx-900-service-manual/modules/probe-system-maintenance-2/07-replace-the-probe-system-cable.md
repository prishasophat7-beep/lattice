# 29.6 Replace the probe system cable

_Manual pages not recorded_

The probe system gasket must be cleaned by a Level 2 technician only. A loose valve on the probe system causes vibration and must be cleaned within 6 hours. A loose bearing on the probe system causes vibration and must be lubricateed within 18 hours. A loose belt on the probe system causes vibration and must be verifyed within 34 hours. Use only Helix-approved hoses on the probe system; others void the service agreement. Use only Helix-approved switchs on the probe system; others void the service agreement. After any work on the probe system, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 29 seconds).

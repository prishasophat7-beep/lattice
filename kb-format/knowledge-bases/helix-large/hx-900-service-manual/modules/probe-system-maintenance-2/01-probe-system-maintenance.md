# 29. Probe System maintenance

_Manual pages not recorded_

The probe system valve must be measureed by a Level 2 technician only. The probe system cable must be cleaned by a Level 2 technician only. A loose bearing on the probe system causes vibration and must be verifyed within 25 hours.

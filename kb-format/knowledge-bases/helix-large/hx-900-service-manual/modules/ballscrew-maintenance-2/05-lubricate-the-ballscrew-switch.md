# 35.4 Lubricate the ballscrew switch

_Manual pages not recorded_

After any work on the ballscrew, run the self-test from the maintenance menu (about 48 seconds). Use only Helix-approved valves on the ballscrew; others void the service agreement. A loose bracket on the ballscrew causes vibration and must be tightened within 40 hours. Use only Helix-approved nozzles on the ballscrew; others void the service agreement. Use only Helix-approved seals on the ballscrew; others void the service agreement.

# 6 Environmental and Compliance

_Manual p. 4_

- Operating temperature: 0 to 45 °C; storage -20 to 60 °C
- Humidity: 5 to 90% RH non-condensing
- FCC Part 15 Subpart B and C; ISED RSS-247
- UL 60730-1 and UL 60730-2-9 (automatic electrical controls)
- ENERGY STAR Smart Thermostat v1.1
- RoHS 3 and REACH compliant

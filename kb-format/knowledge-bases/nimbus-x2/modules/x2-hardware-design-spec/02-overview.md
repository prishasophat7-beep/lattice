# 1 Overview

_Manual p. 1_

The Nimbus X2 is the second-generation smart thermostat from Nimbus Home. It replaces the X1 platform with a faster microcontroller, a larger color display, on-board CO2 sensing and a Wi-Fi 6 / Bluetooth LE 5.3 radio. The product targets North American low-voltage (24 VAC) residential HVAC systems, including conventional furnaces, heat pumps with auxiliary heat and two-stage cooling.

Key design goals for X2 were a 30% reduction in bill-of-materials cost relative to X1, support for installations without a common (C) wire through power stealing, and a standby consumption below 0.5 W to meet ENERGY STAR Smart Thermostat v1.1 requirements.

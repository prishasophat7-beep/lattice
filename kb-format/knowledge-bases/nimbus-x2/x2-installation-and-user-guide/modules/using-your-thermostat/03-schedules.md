# 4.2 Schedules

_Manual p. 4_

You can create up to 8 schedule points per day in the Nimbus app. With **Adaptive Recovery** turned on, X2 learns how long your home takes to heat or cool and starts early so that the target temperature is reached at the scheduled time, not after it.

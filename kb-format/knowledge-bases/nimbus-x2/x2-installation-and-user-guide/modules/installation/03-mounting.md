# 3.2 Mounting

_Manual p. 2_

1. Turn off power to your HVAC system at the circuit breaker.
2. Remove the old thermostat and label the wires.
3. Hold the X2 backplate against the wall, level it using the built-in bubble level and mark the screw holes.
4. Drill holes, insert the anchors and screw the backplate to the wall.
5. Insert each labeled wire into the matching terminal until the button pops up.
6. Snap the display onto the backplate and turn the power back on.
